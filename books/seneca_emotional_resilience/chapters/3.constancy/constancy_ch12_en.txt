[Chapter 12]

The wise person deals with all these people using the exact same mindset we use for young boys. He sees that their childhood still lingers within them, even long after their youth has passed and their hair has turned gray. Growing old does a person no good if their mind still holds all the faults of childhood, and if those flaws have only grown worse over time. These men differ from children only in their size and outward appearance. They are just as unsteady, just as fickle, and they blindly chase pleasure. They only remain quiet out of fear, not out of true self-control.

You cannot say these men are different from children just because children are greedy for toys, nuts, and pennies, while these men are greedy for gold, silver, and entire cities. Children play at being politicians. They pretend to wear the purple-edged robes of magistrates, carry the guards' axes, and sit on the judge's seat. These men do the exact same things in earnest out in the public squares and courts. Children pile up sand on the beach to build houses. Meanwhile, these men wear serious faces as if they are doing important business, but they are just busy piling up stones, walls, and roofs. They do this until they turn what should be a house to protect the body into a weapon that threatens it.

Children and older men make the exact same mistakes. The only difference is that the older men deal with different and more dangerous things. Therefore, the wise person is completely justified in treating the insults he receives from such men as simple jokes. Sometimes he even corrects them with pain and punishment, just as he would discipline children. He does this not because he was hurt, but because they caused harm, and he wants to stop them from doing it again. In the same way, we break in animals with a whip. We do not get angry with the animal if it refuses to carry a rider. We just control it so that the pain will break its stubbornness.

You might wonder why the wise person punishes those who commit insults or injuries when he cannot actually be hurt by them. He is not taking revenge. He is simply correcting them.
