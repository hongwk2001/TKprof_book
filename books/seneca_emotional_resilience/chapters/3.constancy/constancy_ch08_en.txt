Moreover, justice can never suffer anything unjust, because opposites cannot exist together. Since an injury can only be given unjustly, an injury cannot be given to the wise man. You should also not be surprised that no one can injure him, because no one can actually do him a true favor either. The wise man does not lack anything that he could accept as a gift, and a bad person has nothing valuable enough to give him. After all, a person must own something before they can give it to someone else, and a bad person does not own anything that the wise man would gladly accept.

As a result, no one can do harm or good to the wise man. Holy things do not need help, and they cannot be injured. The wise man is very, very close to the gods. He is exactly like a god in every way except that he will eventually die. He moves forward toward a life that is high, orderly, and free from fear. He walks a calm and steady path, meant for the good of mankind, and useful to both himself and others. That is why he will never desire anything low or bad, or cry over it.

A person who relies on reason and navigates human life with a godlike mind has no weak points. He cannot be injured by anyone. I do not just mean that he is safe from other people. He cannot even be injured by the goddess of Fortune, because she always loses and retreats whenever she fights against virtue.

If we can accept death with a calm and unshaken mind, we have conquered the greatest fear of all. Beyond death, angry laws or cruel rulers have nothing left to threaten us with. That is the absolute limit of the goddess of Fortune's power. If we know that death is not an evil, and therefore not an injury, we will be able to handle everything else much more easily, such as losses, pains, shame, moving to a new home, losing loved ones, and saying goodbye. All these things could hit the wise man at the same time and still not overwhelm him, so he definitely does not grieve when they attack him one by one. If the wise man calmly bears the injuries from the goddess of Fortune, he will bear the injuries from powerful people even more easily, because he knows they are just the hands of the goddess of Fortune.
