[Chapter 6]

You may still ask why God allows bad things to happen to good people. The truth is, God does not allow it. He removes all genuine evil from their lives—such as crimes, shameful wickedness, reckless thoughts, selfish schemes, blind lust, and greed. He protects and saves them. No one expects God to also look after their material belongings. Good people leave the management of those minor details to God and reject flashy external things. Democritus actually gave up his wealth because he considered it a burden to a virtuous mind. It is no surprise when God allows things to happen to good people that they would willingly choose for themselves.

You say good people lose their children. This is expected, considering that good people have historically sacrificed their own children for the sake of justice. They are banished, but they often leave their homeland willingly, knowing they will never return. They are killed, but they sometimes choose to end their own lives. They endure certain tragedies simply to teach others how to be strong. They were born to serve as role models for all of humanity.

Imagine God saying to you: "You chose righteousness, so you have no reason to complain against me. I have surrounded other people with fake blessings, and deceived their empty minds with a long and misleading dream. I decorated them with gold, silver, and ivory, but there is no real goodness inside them. If you could see the hidden lives of those you consider lucky, you would realize they are miserable, petty, and hollow. They are decorated only on the outside, just like the walls of their houses. Their good fortune is not genuine. It is merely a thin shell. As long as they can stand tall and show off, they shine and fool people. But when something shakes them and strips away their masks, you see the deep rot hidden beneath all that fake magnificence.

"To you, I gave sure and lasting good things. They only grow better the closer you look at them. I gave you the power to laugh at danger and conquer passion. Your good qualities are all turned inward, so you do not shine on the outside. The universe itself ignores what is outside it and rejoices in itself. I have placed every good thing within your own breast. It is your good fortune not to need any good fortune.

"You might say that sad, dreadful, and hard things happen to you. Since I could not remove these from your path, I gave your mind the strength to fight them. Bear them bravely. In this, you can surpass God himself. God is beyond suffering evil, but you are above it. Despise poverty, for no one lives as poor as they were born. Despise pain, because either it will stop, or you will stop. Despise death, for it either ends you or takes you elsewhere. Despise fate, because I have given her no weapon that can reach your mind.

"Above all, I have made sure that no one can hold you captive against your will. The way of escape is open to you. If you do not choose to fight, you can flee. This is why I made dying the easiest of all essential things. I have set human life on a steep mountain side, where it can easily slip down. Just look, and you will see a short and ready path to freedom. I have not made leaving the world as slow as entering it. If a person died as slowly as they were born, fate would have a huge advantage over you.

"Let every time and place teach you how simple it is to renounce nature and return her gifts. Learn how to die even while praying for life at the altar. Fat oxen fall dead from a tiny wound, and a blow from a human hand can drop a strong animal. When the joint connecting the head and neck is cut, the massive body falls. The breath of life is not seated deep within us. It does not require a sword plunged to the hilt. Death is near the surface. You can pierce the body anywhere you please. The act of dying is too quick to measure. Whether a knot crushes your windpipe, water stops your breathing, a fall crushes you on the ground, or a lungful of fire stops your breath, death acts swiftly. It is foolish to spend so much time dreading something that takes only a second to happen."
