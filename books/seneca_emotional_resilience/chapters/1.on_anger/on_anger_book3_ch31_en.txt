[Chapter 31]

No one is ever satisfied with their own situation if they are constantly staring at what someone else has. This jealousy leads us to become angry even with the gods. We get angry because someone else got ahead of us, entirely forgetting how many people we are already ahead of. We forget that if a person envies only a few people above them, there must be a massive crowd of people behind them envying them. Human nature is so greedy that no matter how much people receive, they feel cheated if they think they could have gotten even more.

Someone might complain, "He made me a praetor, but I really hoped to be a consul. Yes, he gave me the highest honors, but he didn't make me a regular consul. He let me name the year after myself, but he didn't help me become a priest. I was elected to the council, but only to one of them. He gave me every public honor available, but he didn't add anything to my personal bank account. Whatever he gave me, he had to give to someone anyway, and he didn't even pay for it out of his own pocket."

Instead of talking like this, simply thank the person for what you have already received. Be patient for the rest, and be glad that you are not yet so full that you cannot accept more. There is a real joy in having something left to look forward to. If you are chosen over everyone else, be happy that you hold the first place in your friend's heart. If many others were chosen before you, remember how many more people are still below you. Your biggest mistake is that you keep your mental accounts incorrectly. You put a huge value on what you give to others, but a very low value on what you receive from them.
