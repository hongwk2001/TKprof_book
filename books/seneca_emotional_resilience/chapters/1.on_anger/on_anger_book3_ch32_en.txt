[Chapter 32]

Let the different qualities of people stop you from fighting with them. Let us be afraid to be angry with some, ashamed to be angry with others, and simply too proud to be angry with the rest. We certainly look like "great" people when we send a miserable slave to the punishment house! There is no reason to be in such a huge rush to beat him right away, or to break his legs on the spot. We won't lose our precious power if we just wait a little while before using it. We must wait until our minds are clear enough to give rational orders, because right now, we are just blindly following our anger.

When the anger has finally faded, we will see the actual amount of damage that was done. This is the area where we make the most mistakes. We jump straight to the sword, the death penalty, chains, prison, and starvation, all to punish a minor mistake that only deserved a light slap.

You might ask, "How do you expect me to look at the things that hurt me, so that I can see how pathetic and childish they really are?"

Above all else, I urge you to adopt a great and noble mindset. Look down from a higher perspective and see how cheap and dirty all these arguments are. We fight and run around until we are completely out of breath over things that are simply worthless. To anyone who holds truly great and noble ideas, these petty arguments are not even worth a single thought.
