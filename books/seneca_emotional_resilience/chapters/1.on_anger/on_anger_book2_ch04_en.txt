[Chapter 4]

To understand how passions begin, swell, and gain momentum, you must know that the first feeling is involuntary. It acts as a preparation or warning sign for the passion to come.

The second stage comes with a kind of desire, even if it is not stubborn. You might think, "I have been harmed, so it is my duty to seek revenge," or, "This person committed a crime, so they deserve to be punished."

The third stage is completely out of our control. This stage entirely ignores reason and seeks only revenge, regardless of whether it is justified or not.

Just as we cannot avoid physical reflex reactions, we cannot use reason to avoid that first mental impression. We cannot stop ourselves from yawning when we see someone else yawn, and we cannot help blinking when a finger suddenly flies toward our eyes. Reason cannot overcome these automatic reactions, even though training and constant attention might weaken them. However, true emotions—which are created and ended by the deliberate actions of the mind—are completely different from these reflex reactions.
