[Chapter 4]
There might be some doubt about other emotions, but absolutely no emotion looks more terrifying than anger. We have described the appearance of an angry person in previous books. We described how they look sharp and on edge, sometimes turning pale as the blood rushes inward, and other times looking as if all the heat and fire in their body has rushed to their face. This makes their face turn red as if stained with blood. Their eyes restlessly bulge out as if they might pop from their head, or suddenly freeze in a motionless stare.

Add to this the way they grind their teeth together as if they want to eat someone alive. It sounds exactly like a wild boar sharpening its tusks. Notice the cracking of their joints, the way they unconsciously wring their hands, and how they constantly beat their own chest. Listen to their heavy breathing and deep sighs. Look at their staggering body, their harsh, broken speech, and their trembling lips, tightly pressed together as they hiss out curses.

I swear, no wild beast looks as horrifying as an angry human. Not a starving beast, not an animal with a weapon stuck in its heart, and not even a dying animal taking its last breath to bite the hunter. If you have the time, just listen to the words and threats they spit out. The language pouring from their tormented mind is truly awful! If people could clearly see that anger starts by hurting the angry person first, everyone would want to drop it. Some people use anger as a powerful driving force. They see it as a sign of strength and believe that quick revenge is one of the greatest blessings of success. I must warn those people: a person who is a slave to their own anger is never truly strong or even free. I must also warn hardworking and careful people. While other bad habits usually only affect people with weak characters, anger slowly takes over even smart and sensible minds. It takes control so subtly that some people even claim anger is a sign of honesty, leading to the common misunderstanding that the best-natured people are actually the most prone to anger.
