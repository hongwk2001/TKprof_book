[Chapter 34]

Therefore, we must hold back our anger, whether the person who provokes us is on our level, above us, or below us. A fight with your equal is completely unpredictable. A fight with your superior is foolish. A fight with someone beneath you is pathetic. Turning around to bite the one who bit you is the act of a miserable, lowly person. Even mice and ants will bare their teeth if you poke them, and all weak creatures feel attacked the moment they are touched. It will help calm our temper if we try to remember any kind things the person who made us angry might have done for us in the past. Let their past good deeds cancel out their current offense. Let us also think about the honor and respect we will earn when people hear we chose to forgive, and how many valuable friends we can make just by showing mercy.

One of the lessons we can learn from the terrible cruelty of the Roman dictator Sulla is that we should never be angry with the children of our enemies, whether they are political rivals or personal ones. Sulla actually forced the sons of his enemies into exile. There is nothing more unfair than forcing someone to inherit their father's fights. Whenever we feel unwilling to forgive someone, we should ask ourselves if it would really benefit us if everyone in the world were completely unforgiving. The person who refuses to grant a pardon often ends up begging for one later. People who kick others away often find themselves groveling at the feet of those exact same people.

There is no greater glory to be won than by turning anger into friendship. The most loyal allies of the great Roman Empire are the very people who were once its most stubborn enemies. The empire would not be what it is today if wise leaders had not joined the conquerors and the conquered together. If someone is angry with you, respond to their anger with kindness. A fight that is only fought by one side quickly falls apart. It takes two people to fight. But suppose there is a fierce, angry struggle from both sides. Even then, the better person is the one who steps back first. The winner of that fight is actually the real loser. If he strikes you, just step away. If you strike him back, you give him both the opportunity and the excuse to hit you again, and you will not be able to walk away from the fight whenever you please.
