[Chapter 42]

Let us break free from this evil. Let us clear it from our minds and rip it out by the roots. Anger is a weed that will grow back if even the tiniest piece is left behind. Let us not try to control our anger; let us get rid of it completely. There is no such thing as a "moderate" evil habit. We will succeed if we simply put in the effort. The most helpful thing we can do is constantly remember that we are mortal.

Everyone should say to themselves and to their neighbors, "We are not going to live forever. We should not waste our tiny, short lives declaring war on each other. The days that we could spend in honorable happiness should not be wasted on grieving and torturing other people. Life is too precious to waste, and we do not have any spare time to throw away. We have no reason to rush into fights or go out of our way to start arguments. We are weak, fragile creatures. We should not take on massive feuds or use all our energy to tear other people down."

Very soon, a fever or some other sickness will make us too weak to fight these hateful battles. Death will soon step in and separate even the strongest fighters. We should not cause chaos or spend our lives rioting. Fate hangs right over our heads. It crosses off our days one by one, and it is constantly drawing closer. The exact day you mark for your enemy's death might actually turn out to be your own.
