[Chapter 22]

These lessons, however, are meant for raising our children. For ourselves as adults, the circumstances of our birth and past education are already set. We can no longer undo past mistakes or change how we were raised, so we must deal with whatever comes next. Now, we have to fight the very first causes of evil. Anger begins with the belief that someone has treated us unfairly. Therefore, we should not accept this belief too easily. Even when a wrong seems clear and obvious, we must not let our anger explode. Some lies dress up to look exactly like the truth. We should always wait and let time pass, because time eventually reveals what is real. We must not be quick to listen to mean rumors or gossip. Let us recognize and guard against a major flaw in human nature: we are often eager to believe the things we hate to hear, and we get angry before we even have all the facts. It is not just gossip that shakes us; simple suspicion can do the same. We often explode at totally innocent people just because we misread a simple look or a smile and imagine the worst possible meaning. Therefore, we must act as a defender for the person who is not there to speak for themselves, and we must put our anger on hold. You can always dish out a delayed punishment later, but once you punish someone, you can never take it back.
