[Chapter 34]

Now let us look at the other causes of anger. They include food, drink, and all the fancy equipment that goes with them. They include words, insults, disrespectful body language, petty suspicions, stubborn farm animals, lazy slaves, and badly interpreting other people's words. People get so easily offended that they even treat the natural gift of human language as if it were a weapon used against them.

Believe me, the things that make us so incredibly hot-headed are just silly trifles. They are the exact same things that children fight and scream over. There is absolutely nothing serious or important in any of the things that give us such gloomy faces. I will say it again: your anger and madness come directly from putting too much value on worthless things. Someone will say, "This person tried to steal my inheritance! That one falsely accused me in front of the people I was trying so hard to impress! That one tried to steal my mistress!"

People often desire the exact same things. This shared interest should create a bond of friendship, but instead, it becomes a source of endless arguments and hatred. A narrow path always causes people to bump and shove each other as they walk up and down. A wide, spacious road can be used by entire communities without a single person bumping into another. Your shiny objects of desire always cause fights among those who want them. This happens simply because they are small, petty things that cannot be given to one person without being taken away from someone else.
