[Chapter 19]

A hot mind is naturally the most prone to anger. The world is made of four elements, which are fire, air, earth, and water. These elements have matching powers, which are hot, cold, dry, and moist. The mix of these elements creates the variety of regions, animals, bodies, and characters. Our personalities lean one way or another based on which element is strongest inside us. That is why we call certain places wet, dry, warm, or cold. The same differences apply to animals and people. The amount of moisture or heat inside a person makes a huge difference. A person's character takes on the traits of the element they have the most of. A warm mental temper makes someone quick to anger, because fire is active and full of energy. On the other hand, a cold nature makes a person fearful, since coldness is dull and makes things shrink. This is why some Stoic philosophers think anger starts when the blood boils around the heart. The chest is considered the home of anger simply because it is the warmest part of the human body.

People with more moisture in their bodies get angry slowly. They do not have heat ready to use, and they only build it up through movement. Because of this, the anger of women and children is sharp rather than strong, and it sparks easily over minor things. During the dry years of middle age, anger is violent and strong, but it does not grow or last long. As a person's heat fades away, coldness takes its place. Older people are often irritable and complain a lot. The same is true for sick people, those recovering from illness, and anyone whose heat is drained from exhaustion or blood loss. People suffering from thirst or hunger are in the same state, along with those who are naturally pale or weak from a poor diet. Wine fuels anger because it adds heat to the body. Depending on a person's nature, some fly into a rage when they are completely drunk, while others get angry after just a few drinks. For the same reason, people with blonde hair and a red complexion have very hot tempers. They are naturally the color that other people turn when they are furious, and their blood is hot and easily excited.
