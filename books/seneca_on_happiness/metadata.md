## Description
In *Of a Happy Life* (De Vita Beata), Seneca addresses his brother Gallio on the most fundamental of human desires: how to live a happy, fulfilling life. Rejecting the crowd's search for pleasure and external validation, Seneca argues that true happiness is found solely in virtue, reason, and self-sufficiency. In the latter half of the essay, he offers a brilliant defense of the philosopher's relationship with wealth, explaining how the wise man utilizes fortune without being enslaved by it. This premium edition presents Aubrey Stewart's classic translation formatted for modern digital reading.
