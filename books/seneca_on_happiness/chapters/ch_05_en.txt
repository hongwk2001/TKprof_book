[Chapter 5]
I have started to define things without being tied to every single word. We can call a person happy if Reason saves him from hoping or fearing anymore. But rocks and animals also do not feel fear or sadness. No one would call them happy when they do not even understand what happiness is. We can put people in this same category if a dull nature and lack of self-awareness reduce them to the level of beasts. There is no difference between these people and animals. Animals have no Reason at all. These people only have a corrupt, crooked, and sneaky Reason that hurts themselves. No one outside the influence of truth can be called happy. Therefore, a happy life is based on unchanging, true, and reliable judgment. The mind must avoid wounds and even the smallest scratches. It must always keep its position and defend it against the angry attacks of fortune. Only then is the mind unpolluted and free from all evil. Sensual Pleasure surrounds us on all sides. It uses every kind of attack. It tries to win over the mind with sweet words. It tries every imaginable trick to tempt our whole self or parts of us. But would anyone with even a small trace of human nature want to tickle himself with Pleasure day and night? Would he abandon his mind and focus only on bodily joys?
