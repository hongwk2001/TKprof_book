[Chapter 24]
A person makes a mistake if he believes that giving is an easy thing. It comes with very great difficulties if we want to give our favors reasonably instead of randomly throwing them on impulse. I serve this person. I repay the kindness that person gave me. I help this person because I pity him. I help that person to teach him that poverty should not crush him or bring him down. I will give nothing to some people even if they are needy. This is because they will still be needy even if I give them something. I will offer my favor first to some people. I will force it on some people. I can never ignore my own interests while doing this. More people owe me when I am giving something away than at any other time. You will ask if I give to get it back. At least I do not give to throw my favor away. What I give must be placed where it can return to me even if I cannot demand it back. A favor must be invested like a treasure buried deep in the ground. It must be like a treasure you will not dig up unless it is absolutely necessary. Look at how many chances for giving the house of a rich man provides! Who would think we should only show a generous attitude to people wearing fine clothes? Nature orders humanity to do good. What difference does it make if they are slaves or free people? What difference does it make if they were born free or freed later? What difference does it make if their freedom was gained legally or given by agreement among friends? There is a chance to give a favor wherever there is a human being. Therefore, money can be distributed even inside one's own doorstep. You can find a stage to practice generosity there. Generosity receives this name because it comes from a free-born mind, not because it is our duty to a free person. This generosity never goes to mean and unworthy receivers for a wise person. It is never exhausted. It overflows as if its storehouse has not shrunk whenever it finds a worthy target. Therefore, you have no reason to misunderstand the honorable, brave, and energetic language you hear from people studying wisdom. Observe this above all. A person studying wisdom is not the same as a person who has made himself perfect in wisdom. The first person will tell you this. "I express the most admirable feelings in my speech. But I am still struggling in the middle of countless evils. You must not force me to act according to my rules. I am currently forming myself, molding my character, and striving to raise myself to the height of a great example. You may demand that my words and actions match if I ever succeed in finishing everything I decided to achieve." But a person who has reached the top of human perfection will treat you differently and say this. "First, you do not have the right to sit and judge people better than yourself. I have already gained one proof of my righteousness by becoming the target of the hate of bad people. But let me give you a reasonable answer that I would gladly give to anyone. Listen to what I declare and how I value all things. Wealth is not a good thing. It would make people good if wealth were a good thing. But something that can be found even among bad people can never be called a good thing. So I do not allow wealth to be called that. Even so, I admit that wealth is desirable and useful, and it brings great comfort to our lives."
