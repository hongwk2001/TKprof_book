You do not understand this fact, and you are making a face that does not fit your condition. You are like many people who sit in a circus or a theater without knowing their own houses are already holding a funeral. But I look out from a high view. I can see what kind of storm is threatening you. It will crash fiercely over you soon. It has already come close to you and is trying to sweep away everything you own. You probably hardly notice it. But even at this moment, a spinning hurricane is turning like a top and confusing your minds. It makes you pursue the same things and then avoid them. Sometimes it lifts you to high places and then throws you back down. Does it not do this?
