Chapter 5

I have begun to make my definitions without strictly sticking to the exact words. We can call a person happy if Reason helps them cease to hope or fear. Rocks and cattle also feel neither fear nor sadness. Yet, no one calls things happy when they cannot comprehend happiness. You can group them with people whose dull nature and lack of self-knowledge reduces them to the level of animals. No difference exists between them. Animals have no Reason. Those people have only a corrupted form of Reason that twists and uses cunning to harm them. No one achieves happiness when they stay beyond the influence of truth. Consequently, a happy life remains unchangeable and builds upon true and trustworthy judgment. The mind becomes pure and escapes from all evils only when it escapes from both wounds and scratches. It must always maintain its position and defend it even against the angry assaults of Fortune. What about sensual pleasures? Even if they surrounded us on every side and used every means to win over the mind by caresses, no human would want tickles day and night. No one would neglect their mind to devote themselves to bodily enjoyments.
