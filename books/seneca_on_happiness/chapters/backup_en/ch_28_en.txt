Chapter 28

You do not understand this. You bear a countenance which does not befit your condition. You resemble many people who sit in the circus or the theater. They sit there without knowing that their home already remains in mourning. But I, looking forward from a lofty standpoint, can see the storms which threaten you. They will burst in torrents upon you somewhat later. Or they remain close upon you and stand on the point of sweeping away all that you possess. You barely know it. A whirling hurricane at this moment spins round and confuses your minds. It makes them seek and avoid the very same things. It now raises them aloft and now dashes them below.
