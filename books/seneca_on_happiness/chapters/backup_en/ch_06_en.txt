Chapter 6

Someone might say that the mind also has its own pleasures. Let the mind have them, then. Let it judge luxury and pleasures. Let it indulge itself fully in all the things that give sensual delights. Then let it look back upon its past enjoyments. Let it rejoice with all those faded feelings fresh in its memory. Let it look eagerly forward to those other pleasures that it experienced long ago and intends to experience again. The body lies in helpless fullness in the present. At the same time, let the mind send its thoughts onward toward the future and take stock of its hopes. All this makes it appear even more wretched in my opinion. It is crazy to choose evil instead of good. No crazy person can achieve happiness. No one remains sane if they regard harmful things as the Highest Good and strive to obtain them. The happy person makes right judgments in all things. They achieve happiness when they remain satisfied and friendly with their life conditions. This remains true in their present circumstances, whatever they may be. That person achieves happiness when their Reason recommends their entire state of affairs to them.
