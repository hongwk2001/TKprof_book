Chapter 2

When we consider a happy life, you cannot answer me like we count votes. You cannot just say that a view has the most supporters. For that very reason, it is usually the worse opinion. Human affairs lack perfect order, so the majority rarely prefers the better course. The most popular practice is likely the worst practice. Let us therefore ask what is best for us to do, instead of what is most commonly done. We should ask what gives us eternal happiness. We should not care about the crowd's approval. The crowd is the worst possible judge of truth.

By the crowd, I mean both regular people and royalty. I do not judge a person by their clothes. I do not trust my physical eyes to reveal a person's true nature. I have a better and more trustworthy light to distinguish true things from false things. Let the mind discover what is good for the mind.

Sometimes a person gives their mind some breathing room and takes the time to think. Imagine the truths they confess after their own conscience questions them. They say that they wish they could undo their past actions. When they reflect on their words, they envy mute people. They realize their enemies would have wished upon them everything they longed for. Their past fears now seem much more endurable than their past lusts. They have fought with many people. They have turned their dislike of them into friendship, if friendship can even exist between bad people. Yet they have not yet made peace with themselves.

They might say they strove with all their strength to rise above the crowd. They wanted to make themselves famous for some talent. But they accomplished nothing. They only made themselves a target for their enemies. They showed their haters exactly where to strike. Do you see the people who praise your speaking skills, desire your wealth, or boast of your power? All of them either are your enemies, or could easily become your enemies. The number of people who envy you equals the number of people who admire you. Why do I not instead seek some genuine good that I can use and feel? Why do I seek something I merely show off? People marvel at these flashy things and point them out to one another. They look brilliant on the outside. However, on the inside they bring misery to their owners.
