Chapter 16

True happiness consists in Virtue. What will this Virtue bid you do? It will bid you to think nothing bad or good unless it connects with Virtue or wickedness. Next, it bids you to endure the assaults of evil unmoved. As far as is right, it bids you to form a god out of good things. What reward does she promise you for this campaign? She promises an enormous reward. This reward raises you to the level of the gods. You shall suffer no restraint and no want. You shall remain free, safe, and unhurt. You shall fail in nothing that you attempt. You shall lack nothing. Everything shall turn out according to your wish. No misfortune shall befall you. Nothing shall happen to you except the things you expect and hope for. Does Virtue alone suffice to make you happy? Why, of course. Consummate and godlike Virtue such as this not only suffices, but more than suffices. When a person stays beyond the reach of any desire, what can they possibly lack? They concentrate all they need in themselves. How can they require anything from without? However, the person who only walks on the road to Virtue needs some favor from Fortune. They may have made great progress along it. Yet they still struggle among mere human interests. They untie that knot and all the bonds which bind them to mortality. What difference exists between them? The difference is that these bonds tie some more or less tightly. Some have even tied themselves with them as well. The person who progresses toward the upper regions and raises themselves upward drags a looser chain. Though not yet free, they remain as good as free.
