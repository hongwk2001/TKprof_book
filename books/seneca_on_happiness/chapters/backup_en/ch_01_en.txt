Chapter 1

Brother Gallio, everyone wants to live a happy life. However, few understand what truly makes life happy. In fact, happiness is very difficult to achieve. Someone strays further away when they eagerly pursue happiness. This is especially true if they take the wrong path. Their speed only carries them further from their goal. This happens because they head in the opposite direction.

Therefore, we must first clearly define our goal. Next, we must consider the quickest path to reach it. Once we find the right path, the journey itself shows us our daily progress. We will see our progress toward the goal that our natural desires push us toward. However, we might wander aimlessly. The shouts and conflicting calls of people pulling us in different directions might guide us. If so, we will waste our short lives in pointless wandering. This remains true even if we work tirelessly day and night to understand things.

So, we should not decide our destination or our path without advice. We need advice from an experienced person. We need someone who has already explored the region we plan to enter. This journey is unlike any other. On other journeys, a clear path and local advice prevent us from getting lost. But here, the most popular paths lead us furthest astray.

Therefore, nothing is more important than refusing to follow the crowd like sheep. We should not go where everyone else goes. We should go where we ought to go. Our obedience to popular opinion causes us the most trouble. We habitually believe that popular things are truly good. We mistake many fakes for genuine good things. We live not by Reason, but by imitating others.

This is why people rush into great masses. They pile up on top of each other. People press against each other in a huge crowd. No one falls without pulling someone else down. The people at the front cause the downfall of the people behind them. You can observe the same thing in human life. No one simply goes wrong by themselves. They always cause and advise another person's wrongdoing.

Following the path of our predecessors harms us. Everyone prefers to believe others rather than form their own opinion. Because of this, we never make a deliberate judgment about life. Instead, some traditional error always traps us and ruins us. We perish because we follow the examples of other people. We could cure this problem if we separated ourselves from the herd. But as things stand, the mob wants to fight against Reason to defend its own mistake.

Consequently, the same thing happens as at elections. The fickle breeze of popular favor shifts. Then, the very people who chose the leaders view them with admiration. The majority decides, and we all approve and disapprove of the same things.
