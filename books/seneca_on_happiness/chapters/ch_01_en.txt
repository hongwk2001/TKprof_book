[Chapter 1]
Brother Gallio, everyone wants to live a happy life. However, people struggle to see exactly what makes a life happy. Reaching happiness is never easy. You will only move further away from happiness if you choose the wrong path. Your rush will only take you further away if your path goes in the wrong direction. Therefore, we must first clearly define our goal. Then we must think about how to reach it most quickly. The journey itself will show us our daily progress if we only move in the right direction. We will realize how close we are to the goal our nature desires. But people shout and lead us in different directions. We will waste our short life in pointless wandering if we wander aimlessly and depend on their noise. We can work day and night to understand things correctly, but it will be useless. So we must decide where to go and which path to take. We absolutely must seek advice from an experienced person who has already explored this area. This journey has different conditions from other journeys. You can ask the locals about known paths and avoid losing your way on a normal road. But the most crowded and popular road will lead us astray the most on this journey. So it is very important not to act like a sheep that blindly follows the flock ahead. We should not go where others go. We must go where we are supposed to go. Nothing puts us in deeper trouble than trusting public rumors. We accept popular things as the best. We take many fake things instead of truly good things. We live by copying others instead of using Reason. This habit ruins us. This is why people collapse on top of each other and form a huge pile. No one can fall without pulling someone else down when the crowd pushes against each other in a huge mob. The people in front cause the ruin of the people behind them. We can see this exact same thing in human life. No one wanders into the wrong path completely alone. A person always encourages and advises the bad actions of others. Following the line of people ahead is harmful. Everyone trusts the words of others instead of forming their own opinion. So we never make careful judgments about life. Instead, some traditional mistake always traps us and leads us to ruin. We perish because we follow the examples of others. We could cure this disease if we separated ourselves from this crowd. But in reality, the crowd is ready to fight against Reason to defend its mistakes. As a result, public support changes its mind during elections. The same people who elected high government officials and judges later hate them. Every decision made by the voice of the majority ends the same way. We all approve the same things and then reject the same things.
