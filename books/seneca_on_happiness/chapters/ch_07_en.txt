[Chapter 7]
Some people argue that the Highest Good is in the belly. But even they know they give the Highest Good a very shameful position. This is why they say that Pleasure cannot exist separately from Virtue. They argue that no one can live happily without living honorably. They say no one can live honorably without living happily. I do not know how anyone can connect these two completely different things. I ask you what exactly stops Virtue from existing separately from Pleasure. Of course, they say this because all good things come from Virtue. So they believe that even the Pleasure you value and pursue originally comes from the root of Virtue. We would not see things that are pleasant but not honorable if those two things can never separate. We also would not see things that are very honorable but so harsh that we can only get them through pain. In addition, Pleasure visits the most shameful life. But Virtue cannot exist together with an evil life. Yet some unhappy people do not live without Pleasure. In fact, that Pleasure itself makes them unhappy. This could not happen if Pleasure connected to Virtue in any way. On the other hand, Virtue often exists without Pleasure and does not need Pleasure. Why do you try to tie together two things that do not look alike and cannot even match? Virtue is a noble, high, kingly, invincible, and tireless quality. Pleasure is a low, slavish, weak, and easily destroyed thing. It mainly stays in brothels and taverns. You will meet Virtue with dusty, sunburned, and calloused hands. She will be in temples, markets, the senate, and places defending the city walls. On the other hand, you will find Pleasure hiding from the eyes of people. She will be in the dark corners or hot rooms of public baths. She stays in places that fear the visit of inspectors. Pleasure is soft and weak. She smells like wine and perfume. She looks pale or covered in makeup. The Highest Good is immortal. It knows no end. It does not allow complete fullness or regret. A mind that thinks correctly never changes or hates itself. The best things never change. But Pleasure dies at the exact moment it charms us the most. It does not have much room. So it quickly makes us tired and bored. It fades as soon as the first impulse passes. We can never depend on something that changes so easily by nature. Therefore, such a thing appears and disappears very quickly. It even destroys itself through the process of working. It is impossible for any solid substance to exist in this thing. Pleasure reaches a point where it stops existing. It is always looking at its own end from the moment it starts.
