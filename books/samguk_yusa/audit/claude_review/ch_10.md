# ch_10 — Claude review (0 equivalence + 12 quality = 12 items)

Biggest catch: **P0009 n2** invents a whole fictional character and macabre plot beat.
金氏宗財買夫人死 parses as "[a lady of] the Kim clan, **Lady Jaemae**, died" (財買夫人 is
her name+title — matching the valley later being named Jaemaegok after her) — not "Kim
Jongjae purchased [something] concerning his wife." Both the current translation and the
audit's own fix keep the fabricated "purchase" framing; neither noticed there's no
transaction here at all, just someone dying and being buried.

Also recurring: 興虎大王 (P0009 n3) is the same 虎-for-武 taboo substitution seen in
chapters 2/5 (興虎=興武, King Heungmu) — a 4th instance, still uncaught by either pass.

## REVISE (2)

- **P0009 n2** — see above. Correct: KO "김씨 종족의 재매부인이 죽어 청연 상곡에 장사
  지냈다. 이 때문에 그곳을 재매곡이라 불렀다." EN: "A lady of the Kim clan, Lady Jaemae,
  died and was buried at Cheongyeon Sanggok. For this reason, the place was called
  Jaemaegok."
- **P0009 n3** — two issues in the same sentence: (1) 興虎大王 → 興武大王 (King Heungmu,
  the recurring taboo substitution); (2) 至五十四景明王 means "by the time of the
  **54th king**, Gyeongmyeong" (he was Silla's 54th monarch), not "in the 54th year of
  King Gyeongmyeong's reign" — same structural pattern as ch_04's 第四十九憲康大王.
  Fix: KO "...쉰네 번째 임금 경명왕 때에 이르러 공을 흥무대왕으로 추봉했다." EN: "...By
  the time of the fifty-fourth king, Gyeongmyeong, he was posthumously honored as King
  Heungmu." (rest of the flagged AWKWARD phrasing fix still applies to the earlier part
  of the paragraph.)

Note for the final summary: the "Nth 왕代/至Nth王" = "the Nth *king*," not "the Nth *year*
of that king's reign" pattern has now caused a real error twice (ch_04 Heongang, here
Gyeongmyeong) — worth a dedicated check across the corpus for this construction
specifically, similar to the 虎-substitution sweep.

Total: ACCEPT 10, REVISE 2. (12 items.)
