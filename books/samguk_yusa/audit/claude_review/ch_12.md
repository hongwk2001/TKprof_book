flagged with 공자/Confucius — 三十三天 ("the Heaven of the Thirty-Three," a standard

## REVISE (5)

- **P0002 n2** — the "ministers/officers" plural fix is correct, but there's a bigger,
  uncaught number error in the same sentence: hanmun says 一萬二千八百七人 (12,807), but
  EN says "eleven thousand eight hundred seventy-one" (11,871) and KO itself has a typo
  ("칠흔," not a real number word). Fix: KO "일만 이천팔백칠흔 명" → "일만 이천팔백일곱
  명"; EN → "twelve thousand eight hundred seven."
- **P0003 n3** — 赴臨 is a classical set phrase meaning "to attend a mourning/condolence
  ceremony" (standard usage right after a king's death, which is exactly the context:
  義慈王 had just died in Tang exile) — not a place name "Linzi" (current) or generic
  "the capital" (the audit's fix). Fix: KO "황제는 옛 신하들이 조문하러 오는 것을
  허락했다." EN: "The Emperor permitted the former officials to come and mourn him."
- **P0004 n1, n2** (found via the earlier corpus-wide sweep, not flagged by either audit
  pass) — 文虎王 is 文武王 (King Munmu of Silla, a very prominent historical figure — one
  of the most consequential Silla kings), taboo-substituted the same way as 光虎帝/建虎/
  興虎 elsewhere in this corpus. Fix: KO "문호왕"→"문무왕" (both occurrences), EN "King
  Munho"→"King Munmu" (both occurrences).
- **P0006 n3** — 二三其德 is a classical idiom (from the Shijing) meaning "to waver in
  one's [sworn] good faith," i.e. to break an oath — not "their status would be
  stripped" (the audit's fix) or "virtues diminished" (current). Fix: KO "만약 배신하여
  그 맹세를 저버린다면 군사를 일으켜..." EN: "If there were any betrayal, any breach of
  the sworn oath, armies would be raised and mobilized to invade the borders."
- **P0009 n4** — 王師 here means "the royal/imperial army" (troops, in a
  post-war-campaign context), not a person named "Wangsa" — the audit's note gestures at
  a different, unrelated meaning ("a monk who became the king's teacher") and its
  returned fix is a no-op that doesn't change anything. Fix: KO "왕의 군대가 백제를
  평정하고 돌아오자, 신라 왕은 장수들에게 명령했다..." EN: "After the royal army had
  pacified Baekje and returned, the Silla king ordered his generals..."
- **P0012 n3** — see chapter note above: 三十三天 (the Heaven of the Thirty-Three,
  Trāyastriṃśa) misread as "thirty-three thousand heavens," confusing 天 (heaven) with
  千 (thousand) — the audit's own fix keeps this error. Fix: KO "그때 하늘에서 소리가
  울려 퍼졌다. 도리천, 즉 삼십삼천의 신 한 사람이 신라에 내려와 김유신이 되었다는
  내용이었다." EN: "At that time, a voice echoed from the heavens, proclaiming that one
  of the gods of the Heaven of the Thirty-Three (Trāyastriṃśa) had descended to Silla
  and become Kim Yusin."

Total: ACCEPT 17 (15 + 2 combined equiv groups), REVISE 5 (covering 7 individual
