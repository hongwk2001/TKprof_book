# ch_19 — Claude review (3 equivalence + 22 quality = 25 items)

Note: 王師 appears twice in this chapter with genuinely different meanings depending on
context — P0011 n3 (spiritual/cultural framing, "威靈"/numinous power) is "the royal
preceptor" (a monk-teacher), while P0012 n3 (a king "fighting to the death" against an
approaching force) is "the royal army." The audit correctly resolved the first and
wrongly flipped the second.

## ACCEPT (13)

- **P0002 n2, P0004 n2, P0004 n5, P0005 n4, P0006 n4, P0007 n4, P0009 n1, P0009 n4,
  P0010 n1, P0011 n3, P0011 n4, P0012 n4** — real or harmless fixes.

## REVISE (7)

- **P0003 n3** — 戊子 romanizes as "Muja," not "Mujo" (current KO already correctly has
  "무자"; only EN has the slip, and the audit's own fix doesn't touch it). Fix: EN
  "Mujo"→"Muja."
- **P0004 n3** — 王公 here is a direct parallel to 甄氏 (Gyeon Hwon) two clauses earlier
  — "before, when *Gyeon* came it was like meeting wolves; now that *Lord Wang* [Taejo]
  has arrived, it's like seeing our parents" — a specific-person-to-specific-person
  contrast, not "royalty and lords" as a vague collective (the audit's fix). Keep it
  about Taejo specifically: EN "Now that Lord Wang [Taejo] has arrived, it is like
  seeing one's parents."
- **P0005 n3** — 皆骨山 is a well-attested classical alternate name for Mt. Geumgang
  (금강산/皆骨山, "All-Bones Mountain"), correctly romanized 개골산/Gaegolsan — both
  current ("개산"/"Gaesan," dropping a syllable) and the audit's fix ("Gaesansan") miss
  the "골" syllable. Fix: KO "개산"→"개골산," EN "Gaesan(san)"→"Gaegolsan."
- **P0006 n2** — beyond the flagged 正承/Jeongseung-won naming fix, this sentence also
  has an uncaught error: 謝 ("apologized/felt ashamed") is misread as if it names a clan,
  "사가 가문" ("the Sa clan") — no such clan is mentioned in the hanmun. Fix: KO "왕이
  자기 나라를 떠나 다른 나라에 살게 된 것을 부끄러워하였으므로, 이를 난새에 비유하여
  봉호를 신란공주로 고쳤다." EN: "Because the King felt ashamed at having left his own
  country to live in another, Taejo likened this to a phoenix and changed her title to
  Sinran-gongju." (keep the Jeongseung-won fix from the audit alongside this.)
- **P0009 n3** — 臣翮 is a standard classical self-effacing document signature: 臣
  ("your servant/I") + 翮 (Hyeok, the actual given name) — not a two-syllable name
  "Sin Hyeok." Fix: EN "Hanrim Sin Hyeok" → "Your servant Hyeok, the Hallim scholar."
- **P0010 n=3** — 金傳 is 김부/Kim Bu, this chapter's own protagonist, named as such
  throughout (starting from "第五十六金傳大王" at the very top of the chapter) — current
  KO already correctly says "김부"; only EN has drifted, and the audit's own fix
  ("Geumjeon," misreading it as an action/title) makes it worse, not better. Fix: EN
  "Geumbun"→"Kim Bu" (matching KO and the rest of the chapter).
- **P0012 n=3** — opposite situation from P0011 n3: here 王師 sits in a plainly military
  context (a king "fighting to the death" before his family is "all slaughtered," i.e.
  resisting an approaching army) — current text already correctly says "royal army," and
  the audit's fix ("the Royal Teacher") introduces a wrong reading. Reject the fix, keep
  current.

## REJECT (1)

- **P0013 n3** — 神會/신회 here is a Silla official holding the rank 阿干 (Agan), not the
  famous 8th-century Chinese Chan monk 荷澤神會 (Shenhui) — no connection between a Silla
  court rank and that monk. Keep the Korean reading "Sinhoe."
