# ch_03 — Claude review (3 equivalence + 14 quality = 17 items)

Two flags in this chapter deserve special attention: **P0008 n=1/n=2** is a case where the
model's "fix" would have made a male king character become pregnant and lay an egg — an
absurd regression from blindly applying a wrong TERM_ERROR call. And **P0009 n=4** /
**P0010 n=2** both show the same failure mode: an AWKWARD "smoothing" fix that quietly
drops a whole sentence of real narrative content (the fish-and-turtle bridge scene in
P0010) rather than just rephrasing it. Both patterns are exactly why nothing here should
be applied unreviewed.

## ACCEPT (10)

- **P0002 n3** — minor smoothing, fix_en acceptable.
- **P0004 n1** — the parenthetical gloss says only 東明, not the honorific-expanded
  동명성왕; KO added the honorific. fix correct.
- **P0004 n4, P0008 n3, P0008 n5, P0011 n3** — plain style smoothing, no content risk, fixes fine.
- **P0007 n3** — "obtained"→"encountered" for 得 is a defensible register call, no factual issue.

## REVISE (2) — fix drops real content, replaced with a non-lossy version

- **P0009 n4** — fix_en truncates the paragraph, silently dropping "The king rode the fat
  horse himself, and Jumong was given the lean one" (王自乘肥，瘦者給蒙 — the actual point
  of the whole horse-feeding scheme). Fix: "Jumong identified the swift horses and reduced
  their feed to make them lean, while he raised the slow ones well so they became fat.
  The king rode the fat horse himself, giving Jumong the lean one."
- **P0010 n2** — fix_en drops the fish-and-turtle bridge collapse entirely ("He crossed
  the bridge, but it immediately unraveled. The pursuing horses could not cross.") — a
  memorable plot beat, not filler. Fix: keep it, only correct "above Biryusu"→"along the
  Biryusu River" as flagged: "He crossed the bridge, but it immediately came apart, and
  the pursuing horsemen could not cross. He arrived at Jolbonju (the border of
  Hyeondo-gun) and settled there. Lacking time to build a palace, he built a hut along the
  Biryusu River and lived there. The nation was named Goguryeo."

## REJECT (2)

- **P0008 n1, n2** (both TERM_ERROR) — claims the subject should be "Geum-wa," not
  "Yu-hwa." Wrong: this passage is Yuhwa's own first-person account of her exile (誘我/往
  不返/父母責我 — "lured *me*," "*I* did not return," "my parents scolded *me*"), correctly
  rendered in 3rd person about Yuhwa. n=2 correctly tracks a genuine subject shift *within*
  the hanmun itself (金蛙異之 = Geumwa marvels/confines her → 引身避之 = *she* withdraws
  from the light) — the current KO/EN already gets this right. Applying the suggested fix
  would replace "Yuhwa" throughout, including "Geum-wa became pregnant and laid an egg" —
  Geumwa is the male king established throughout this chapter. No change.
