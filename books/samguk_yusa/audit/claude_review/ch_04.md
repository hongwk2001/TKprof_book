# ch_04 — Claude review (5 equivalence + 27 quality = 32 items)

Heaviest chapter so far (Silla founding myths + a village/mansion-name catalog). Two
structural findings worth flagging beyond the individual items:

1. **`fix_en` is sometimes a full paragraph, sometimes just the corrected clause**, and
   nothing in the data distinguishes which. Applying it as a blind string-replace of the
   whole paragraph will silently delete real content whenever it's the latter (seen
   repeatedly this chapter: P0003n1, P0013n2, P0014n4, P0012n2). Any future apply step
   must never wholesale-replace on a shorter fix_en without checking.
2. Two errors here are bigger than anything either audit pass flagged, found only because
   the same paragraph was already open for review (**P0008 n1**'s season list, **P0013
   n2**'s era name) — both detailed below.

## REVISE (expanded — found while reviewing the flagged item, bigger than what was flagged)

- **P0008 n1** — the actual defect is much larger than "代 means reign, not successor":
  the four-season guardian-house list itself has been corrupted. Hanmun: 春(spring)=東野
  宅, 夏(summer)=谷良宅, 秋(autumn)=仇知宅, 冬(winter)=加伊宅. Current KO/EN shift summer
  and autumn's houses by one season each, and drop winter's guardian (加伊宅) entirely,
  merging it into a garbled sentence about "Gai" becoming "the 49th successor." What
  actually follows (第四十九憲康大王代。城中無一草屋...) is an unrelated new sentence about
  the capital's prosperity under the 49th king, Heongang — not about anyone's succession
  at all. Fix — KO: "봄에는 동야택이, 여름에는 곡량택이, 가을에는 구지택이, 겨울에는
  가이택이 [번갈아] 머물렀다. 마흔아홉째 헌강대왕 때에는 성안에 초가집이 하나도 없었고,
  처마와 담이 서로 이어져 있었으며, 노랫소리와 풍악이 밤낮으로 끊이지 않았다." EN:
  "Dongyataek in spring, Gollyangtaek in summer, Gujitaek in autumn, and Gaitaek in
  winter took their turns. During the reign of the forty-ninth king, Heongang the Great,
  there was not a single thatched house within the walled city; eaves and walls touched
  one another, and song and music filled the streets without ceasing, day and night."
- **P0013 n2** — 地節 (Dijie/지절) is a two-character Han-dynasty era name; the current
  translation splits it, reading 地 as the common noun "땅" (land) and 節 alone as if it
  were the era name "Jeol." fix_en also truncates the paragraph, dropping the six-tribe
  gathering entirely. Fix — KO: "전한 지절 원년 임자년 삼월 초하루에 여섯 부의 조상들이
  각각 자제들을 거느리고 알천 기슭에 모두 모였다..." (rest unchanged). EN: "In the first
  year of the Dijie era of the Former Han, on the first day of the third month of the
  year Imja, the ancestors of the six tribes each led their sons and brothers and
  gathered together on the banks of Alcheon..." (rest unchanged).
