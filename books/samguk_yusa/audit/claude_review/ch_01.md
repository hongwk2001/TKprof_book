## REVISE (2) — real issue, model's fix rejected/replaced

- **P0002 n3** — 河出圖/洛出書: KO/EN both invert the source/product relationship (treat
  Hado/Nakseo as the *place things emerged from*, when they're the chart/document itself,
  which emerged *from* the Yellow River / Nak River — glossary already says this). The
  model's fix just adds "(Luo-shu)" as a gloss and doesn't fix the inversion. My fix — KO:
  "그래서 황하에서 하도가 나오고 낙수에서 낙서가 나온 뒤에 성인들이 나타났다." EN:
  "Therefore, after Hado emerged from the Yellow River and Nakseo emerged from the Nak
  River, sages appeared."
- **P0003 n1** — 龍交大澤 ("a dragon *united with* a great marsh") fits the passage's run of
  divine unions (rainbow/Nüdeng/etc. producing legendary births); the model's fix
  ("emerged from") drops that thread. My fix: "A dragon united with a great marsh and
  fathered Lord Pei."
