# ch_23 — Claude review (8 equivalence + 40 quality = 48 items)

This is the 가락국기 (Garak-guk-gi, King Suro/Gaya founding chronicle) — the largest
chapter. Two catches worth flagging: **P0010 n2** — 臧獲 is a classical term for
*slaves/servants* (a paired counted-in-persons term, "口"), not "stolen goods and game"
(the audit's own fix, still treating people as property/objects); and **P0011 n2** —
二八 is the standard classical age idiom for **16** (2×8), so the *current* "sixteen" was
already correct and the audit's fix ("twenty-eight") is the actual error here.

## REVISE (4)

- **P0010 n2** — see chapter note. Fix: KO "그들은 노비를 합쳐 스무여 명을 거느렸다."
  EN: "Together with their slaves, they numbered more than twenty people."
- **P0012 n3** — 螓首 is a classical beauty idiom (from the Shijing: a woman's smooth,
  broad forehead — cicada-brow), not "shaved head." Both current and the audit's fix
  keep the "shaved head" misreading. Fix: KO "아름다운 이마를 하고 감히 용의 얼굴에
  다가가는 것은 대담한 일이었다." EN: "It was a bold act for one so fair of brow to
  dare approach the dragon's face."
- **P0014 n2** — 汝諧 reads 여해 (諧 = 해), not 여협 — the audit's fix is a no-op that
  doesn't touch the actual error. Fix: KO "여협"→"여해," EN "Yeohyeop"→"Yeohae."
- **P0017 n1** — 后崩 means "the Queen's death" (referring back to Heo Hwangok's own
  death, recounted earlier in this same chapter), not "the concubine's death" (current,
  wrong rank) — and not "the King's passing" either (the audit's fix), which would be
  circular since this whole sentence is already about the king's own death. Fix: KO
  "후궁이 죽었을 때보다"→"왕후가 세상을 떠난 날보다," EN "the concubine's death"→
  "the Queen's passing."

## REJECT (2)

- **P0007 n1** — the fix's only change is adding "(解)" as a parenthetical hanja gloss
  in Korean, which violates house style (no exposed hanja); the English is unchanged.
  No net improvement.
- **P0011 n2** — 二八 is the standard classical idiom for 16 (2×8); current "sixteen"
  is already correct. The fix ("twenty-eight") reads it as a literal number rather than
  the multiplication idiom — a regression.
