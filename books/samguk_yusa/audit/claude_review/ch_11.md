# ch_11 — Claude review (4 equivalence + 25 quality = 29 items)

Two chapter-spanning naming errors here: **虎王 misread as "Horang"/"the Tiger King"**
(same taboo-substitution family as ch_02/05/10 — this one is 虎王=武王, King Mu of
Baekje, Uija's actual father), and **羅人 mistranslated as "Goryeo forces"** three
separate times (P0010 n2/n4/n6) — this passage is entirely about the 660 CE
Baekje–Tang–Silla war, so 羅 here is Silla (as it is everywhere else in this same
chapter, e.g. "羅王"="the Silla king" a few lines earlier), not Goryeo. The audit's own
fix for these three ("Liaodong forces") is a *different* wrong answer, not a correction.

## REVISE (6)

- **P0005 n1** — 虎王 is the taboo-substituted 武王 (King Mu of Baekje, Uija's real
  father), not a literal "Tiger King" — the audit's own fix keeps the literal
  mistranslation. Fix: KO "호왕의 장남" → "무왕의 맏아들," EN → "the eldest son of King
  Mu."
- **P0006 n4** — the flagged issue (佐平書案 = "the chief minister's writing desk," one
  compound phrase, not an object called "Seopyeongan" sitting on a desk) is real, but
  the returned fix_en is a clause from a *different*, later sentence and doesn't touch
  it at all. Fix: KO "그중 한 마리 흰 여우가 좌평의 책상 위에 앉아 있었다." EN: "one white
  fox sat atop the chief minister's writing desk."
- **P0007 n3** — 蝦蟆 is one word meaning "toad," not "shrimp and frogs" (two separate
  creatures). The audit's own fix ("shama") isn't a real word either. Fix: KO "새우와
  개구리" → "두꺼비," EN → "toads."
- **P0008 n5** — 意者國家盛而新羅浸微 (the favorable omen-reading, pleasing to the
  Baekje king) and 太宗聞百濟國中多怪變 (Silla's Taejong separately hearing of Baekje's
  bad omens) are two different clauses about two different kings' reactions, wrongly
  fused into one sentence ("the king was pleased to hear that Taejong had heard...").
  The audit's fix just deletes the Taejong clause instead of splitting it. Fix: KO:
  "그 뜻은 나라가 흥성하고 신라가 점점 쇠약해진다는 것이었다. 왕은 기뻐했다. 그 무렵
  태종은 백제 나라 안에 괴이한 일이 많다는 것을 들었다. 오년 경신년이었다." EN: "This
  meant that the state was flourishing while Silla was gradually declining. The king
  was pleased. Around that time, Taejong heard that there were many strange occurrences
  within Baekje. It was the year Gyeongsin, the fifth year."
- **P0010 n2, n4, n6** (equiv/MISSING on n2 + all 3 quality/TERM_ERROR) — 羅人/羅 here is
  Silla (see chapter note above), not "Goryeo forces" (current) nor "Liaodong forces"
  (the audit's own fix). Correct throughout: KO "고려군"→"신라 사람들/신라군," EN "Goryeo
  forces"→"Silla forces" (never "Liaodong").

## NEEDS-HUMAN (1)

- **P0004 n2** — this whole clause ("庶子曰皆知文級干車得令公馬得阿干") reads more
  naturally as a list of THREE more of Taejong's sons and their ranks — 文級干(Munkeupgan),
  車得令公(Lord Chadeuk), 馬得阿干(Madeuk Agan) — matching the pattern of every other name
  in this same sentence (文姬之所出也: 仁問, 文王, 老旦, 智鏡, 愷元, all named sons with
  ranks) — rather than "Chadeuk obtained [a/the Lord's] horse." Under that reading 馬得
  is a person's name (Madeuk), not "horse" at all. This reframes the sentence
  substantially enough that I'm flagging rather than applying it: candidate — KO: "서자로는
  문급간, 거득영공, 마득아간과 다섯 딸이 있었다." EN: "His children by secondary consorts
  included Munkeupgan, Lord Chadeuk, and Madeuk Agan, along with five daughters."
