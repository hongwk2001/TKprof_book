# ch_05 — Claude review (6 equivalence + 19 quality = 25 items)

**Systemic issue found, not new to this chapter**: 光虎帝/建虎 (the same Goryeo
naming-taboo substitution already established for 周虎王→周武王 in the glossary, i.e.
虎 stands in for 武) recurs here as 光虎帝 (P0012 n1) and 建虎 (P0006 n5, inside the
Dosolga passage) and was **not** caught by either audit pass, same as ch_02 P0009 n3.
This is now three occurrences across two chapters that both passes missed — worth a
one-time grep across the whole corpus for 光虎/建虎/광호/건호 rather than catching them
one at a time as I read (see summary at the end of the full review for this).

## ACCEPT (13)

- **P0009 n1, P0010 n1** — 七寶奴婢 is "seven treasures *and* slaves" (two items), not
  "slaves guarding seven treasures." Fixes correct (minor: still slightly subordinates
  the slaves to the treasures rather than treating them as co-equal cargo, but low
  stakes).
- **P0011 n2** — 長公主 = "the eldest princess" (長 = eldest, not part of a name); current
  text invented a "Princess Jang." Fix correct.
- **P0002 n3, P0002 n4, P0003 n2, P0003 n4, P0004 n1, P0004 n2, P0008 n2, P0011 n3,
  P0011 n4** — real minor fixes (Gapsa→Gapja typo, 辰言王→진언 gloss-not-name, phrasing
  smoothing, 東下→"east" not "southeast"). Apply as **clause-level edits only** — several
  of these fix_en strings are shorter than the source paragraph (same ambiguity issue as
  ch_04: don't wholesale-replace the paragraph, or real content gets silently dropped).

## REVISE (4)

- **P0003 n3** — the note correctly flags that "Mallyukgan"/"Suro" are wrong (should be
  마립간/Marip-gan, and 立's variant 袖 reads 수/Su, not "Suro" — which is a different
  historical king's name bleeding in from elsewhere in the book), but the returned
  fix_en doesn't actually change either word — it's a no-op that repeats the same
  errors. My fix: "Marip-gan (where Marip was sometimes written as Su)."
- **P0006 n2** — "the king" is circular (the whole point of the scene is determining
  *who* becomes king). Fix swaps to "he," equally ambiguous. Use the name: "Norye had
  more teeth, so he was made king first, and thus given the name Isageum."
- **P0008 n3** — 海尺 = "boatman/fisherman" (occupation), not "helped build boats." The
  fix ("mother of the king's fishing boat") is also wrong — a person can't be a boat's
  mother. Correct: "She was the mother of the king's fisherman."
- **P0012 n1** — grammar fix ("was passed away"→"passed away") is correct, but the same
  sentence also has the recurring 光虎帝→광무제 taboo-substitution bug (see note above).
  KO: "노례왕이 세상을 떠나자 광무제 중원 여섯 해 정사년 유월에 왕위에 올랐다." EN:
  "When King Nore passed away, he ascended the throne in the sixth month of the year
  Jeongsa, the sixth year of Emperor Guangwu's Zhongyuan era."

## REJECT (2)

- **P0002 n2** — the note itself suggests "Yun-je" is more accurate for 雲, but 雲's
  standard Sino-Korean reading is 운 (un); the existing "Unje" is already correct.
- **P0009 n3** — current text ("eight ranks of Seonggol, yet without distinction of
  status") already captures 無棟擇 reasonably; the fix's "regardless of selection" is
  vaguer, not an improvement.

## ACCEPT, combined fixes (equivalence items — no audit-generated fix_en existed, mine)

  향가의 격식)을 갖추었다. 처음으로 쟁기와 얼음 창고를 만들었으며, 수레도 만들었다.
  건무(建武) 십팔 년에 이서국을 정벌하여 멸망시켰다. 그해에 고구려 군대가 쳐들어왔다." EN:
  "He first composed the Dosolga, which had a lament-refrain and followed the sanoe
