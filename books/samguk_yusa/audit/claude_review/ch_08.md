# ch_08 — Claude review (6 equivalence + 9 quality = 15 items)

Biggest catch this chapter: **"Wanggi"** (王忌), a named character in the current
translation who menaces Prince Nulji, doesn't exist in the source. 王忌憚 parses as
王(the king) + 忌憚(a two-character verb, "dreaded/feared") — "the king dreaded [Nulji]" —
not "[a person named] Wanggi." Neither audit pass caught this; the quality pass only
fixed the adjacent "Nuljiwang→Nulji" title issue in the same sentence and left the
invented character standing.

## REVISE (2)

- **P0002 n1** — the flagged fix (drop the "-wang" suffix on Nulji, since he's still
  crown prince here) is correct, but leaves the bigger issue untouched: "Wanggi" (see
  above) isn't a person. Fix — KO: "왕이 전왕의 태자 눌지가 덕망이 높음을 두려워하여
  그를 해치려 했다." EN: "The king dreaded Nulji, the former king's crown prince, who
  had great virtue and renown, and sought to harm him."
- **P0005 n3** — deeper than "clunky phrasing": 上亥上子上午 names three *days* in the
  60-day cycle (first Boar-day, first Rat-day, first Horse-day of the month — a
  divination taboo custom), not *hours* of a single day. Both the current text and the
  audit's fix say "hours of the Pig and Rat" (wrong category, and drops 上午 entirely).
  Fix — KO: "백성들은 매달 첫 해일과 첫 자일과 첫 오일에는 모든 일을 삼가고 조심하며
  함부로 움직이지 않았다." EN: "The people refrained from all activity on the first Day
  of the Boar, the first Day of the Rat, and the first Day of the Horse each month."
- **P0007 n1** — "shiro" is a garbled leftover of 諡號 (si-ho, "posthumous title[s]"),
  redundant with the preceding sentence which already says "his posthumous title was
  Jijeung." The audit's fix keeps "shiro" with a gloss bolted on; simpler to just drop
  it: "The custom of posthumous titles began with this king."
