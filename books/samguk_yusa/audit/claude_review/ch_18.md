## REVISE (4)

- **P0002 n=2** (equiv/MISSING) — bigger than either flag catches: 此非王居仁誰作此文 is
  the king's **accusation** ("if this isn't [by] Geoin, who wrote it?"), not a denial
  ("this was not written by Wanggeoin"). The very next sentence has them imprison
  Geoin *because of* this — which only makes sense if the king is naming him as the
  suspect, not clearing him. Fix: KO "왕과 권신들이 그 글을 얻고는 말했다. '거인이
  아니면 누가 이 글을 지었겠는가?'" EN: "The King and the powerful officials obtained
  the text and said, 'If not Geoin, who else could have written this?'"
- **P0003 n=4** — 阿飱良貝王 parses as "Achan (a rank) Yangpae," not "Asinnyangpaewang" —
  阿飱/Achan is a rank used constantly throughout this whole corpus; "Asin" doesn't match
  its reading at all. The audit's own fix just re-hyphenates the same wrong reading.
  Fix: KO "아찬 양패왕" → treat as "Achan Yangpae," EN "Asinnyangpaewang"→"Achan
  Yangpae."
- **P0010 n=2/None** (equiv/SHIFTED+OMITTED = quality/TERM_ERROR on Mo Saji, same tag) —
  two separate omens got merged: 二月 (2nd month, the pagoda-shadow event) and 又十月
  ("separately, in the 10th month," the bowstring event) are two different calendar
  dates for two different events, not "in the 10th month... [then] ten months later."
  Fix: KO "칠 년 경진년 이월에 황룡사 탑의 그림자가 지금의 모 사지 집안에 거꾸로 서
  있기를 한 달간 계속되었다. 또 시월에는, 사천왕사의 다섯 방위 신궁은 줄이 모두
  끊어졌다..." EN: "In the second month of the seventh year, Gyeongjin, the shadow of
  the Hwangnyongsa pagoda stood upside down in the household of Mo Saji for a full
  month. Separately, in the tenth month, the strings of the five directional divine bows
  at Sacheonwangsa all snapped..." (also folds in the Mo Saji fix from the quality
  pass.)
- **P0012 n1/n2** — 百座 is the name of a specific, well-documented Buddhist ceremony
  (a "Hundred-Seat Dharma Assembly," a mass event with many seated monks), not a
  person's name "Baekja" — confirmed by the same passage feeding 300 monks together and
  calling it "the beginning of the general teaching of Seon." The audit's own fix for
  n1 still frames it as a person's role ("the one who preaches the hundred teachings").
  Fix: KO "황룡사에서 백좌법회를 열어 경전을 설법했다... 이 백좌법회는 선종 가르침의
  시작을 알리는 것이었다." EN: "At Hwangnyongsa, a Hundred-Seat Dharma Assembly was
  held to expound the scriptures... This Hundred-Seat Assembly marked the beginning of
  Seon teaching." (Keep n2's other fix — "practiced communal meals" — as-is.)
