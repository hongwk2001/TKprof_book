# ch_09 — Claude review (4 equivalence + 19 quality = 23 items)

Two of the audit's own "fixes" this chapter invent things that are worse than the
original: **P0003 n1** turns a common classical phrase ("as before," 如平昔) into a
fabricated king-name "Jupyeong," and **P0010 n5** takes 閼川 (Alcheon), a real,
documented historical Silla general, and reduces him to a place name.

## ACCEPT (13)

- **P0002 n4, P0002 n5, P0004 n4, P0005 n2, P0005 n4, P0007 n2, P0008 n1, P0008 n2,
  P0012 n3, P0012 n4, P0012 n5** — real or harmless fixes, apply (mostly clause-level;
  check paragraph length before replacing wholesale).
- **P0008 n1** — 丈六尊像 is a specific, famous Buddha-statue type ("Six-span image"), not
  a "tall stele" (a stele isn't a statue at all). Real, good catch.
- **P0010 n2** — 三升 (a small volume unit) mistranslated as "한 말"/"one peck" (~10x
  larger). Fix correct, but its subject is wrong: "**He** sent three seong," not "she" —
  Emperor Taizong is the one sending; use "he."

## REVISE (3)

- **P0002 n2** — KO says "나흘" (four *days*), hanmun says 四年 (four *years*) — a real,
  large unit error (a king's reign length off by three orders of magnitude). No fix_en
  was returned for this one at all; EN is already correct ("four years"), only KO has
  the bug. Fix KO only: "나흘"→"사 년."
- **P0011 n4** (equiv/MISSING = quality/AWKWARD, same tag) — KO "낭산 양쪽" (both sides
  of Nangsan) mistranslates 陽 (south/sunny side, singular) as if it were "양쪽" (both
  sides) — two different words. The audit's fix only touches EN ("sunny side," already
  fine); KO needs its own fix: "낭산 남쪽 기슭에" or "낭산의 양지쪽에."
- **P0013 n2** (equiv/MISSING = quality/TERM_ERROR, same tag) — 良志師傳 = "the biography
  of [Master] Yangji" — 良志 (Yangji) is a documented historical Silla monk-sculptor,
  associated with exactly this kind of temple-building context. Current "Yangsa" drops
  a syllable; the audit's own fix ("Ryongsisa") is a bigger, unrelated fabrication that
  doesn't match 良志's reading at all. Correct: KO "양지 스님의 전기," EN "the biography
  of Yangji."

## REJECT (3)

- **P0003 n1** — "如平昔" is a common classical adverbial phrase ("as before," "as in
  former times"), not a name. The audit's fix invents a king "Jupyeong"/"왕여평" from
  it — a fabrication, and a worse reading than the current text (which, whatever its
  imprecision, at least doesn't invent a nonexistent king).
- **P0010 n5** — 閼川 (Alcheon) is a real, well-documented Silla general (Gakgan rank),
  not a place. The fix ("go to Alcheon," treating it as a destination) is a regression
  that erases a historical figure. No change needed to the Alcheon reference itself —
  see the real, separate error caught below.
- **P0011 n2** — the fix adds "(亐召)" as a parenthetical hanja gloss, which directly
  violates this project's own style rule (never expose hanja in the Korean text, no
  parenthetical hanja). Current "Soso" is fine as-is.

## NEEDS-HUMAN (2)

- **P0004 n=None** (equiv/OMITTED) — two of Iryeon's own location/variant-name glosses
  dropped: "(在京城西)" and "(一作神眾寺。誤一云荒川東深渠)". I don't have paragraphs 1–3
  of this tag in this extraction to know exactly where each clause should be spliced
  in, so flagging rather than guessing blind. Candidates: after the riverbank mention,
  "(도성 서쪽에 있다)"/"(west of the capital)"; after the bridge-site name, "(신중사라고도
  하나 잘못이다. 어떤 이는 황천 동쪽 깊은 개천이라고도 한다)"/"(also called Sinjungsa,
  though mistakenly; some say it was the deep channel east of Hwangcheon)".
- **P0003 n5** — 差執事 could be a compound office title ("Chajipsa," a specific Silla
  rank) or 差 could just be the verb "assigned to" + the office 執事 (jipsa). The audit's
  own suggested romanization ("Chisa-jipsa") is inconsistent with 差's reading (cha, not
  chi), so I don't trust it either way without checking the Silla office-rank record.
