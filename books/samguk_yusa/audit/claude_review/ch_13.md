## REVISE (5)

- **P0006 n=3** (equiv/EXTRA = quality/TERM_ERROR, same tag) — 圓扉 ("round gate") is a
  classical literary term for **prison**, not a person's name "Wonbi." This reading is
  corroborated later in the same chapter (P0010: "仁問在獄時" — "while Inmun was
  imprisoned" — matching this exact period). Fix: KO "그러고는 인문을 옥에 가두고,
  오십만 군사를 훈련시켜 설방을 대장으로 세워 신라를 치려 했다." EN: "He then had Inmun
  thrown into prison, and trained five hundred thousand soldiers, appointing Xue Fang as
  commander to attack Silla."
- **P0007 n5** — 瑜珈 (Yoga/esoteric-tantric tradition) describes the *type of monks*
  ("twelve adept monks of the Yoga tradition"), not an object they're "holding" — both
  the current text ("prayer beads") and the audit's own fix ("yugwa," still framed as a
  held object) miss this. Fix: KO "그는 유가(밀교) 승려 열두 명을 불러 모았다..." EN:
  "He summoned twelve monks of the esoteric Yoga tradition..."
- **P0009 n1** — beyond the flagged temple-name fix (Sacheonwangsa, not "Sinsa
  Cheonwangsa"), the mountain name is also wrong: hanmun says 狼山 (Nangsan), matching
  this same chapter's own P0007 ("낭산 남쪽에 신유림이..."), but this paragraph says
  "남산"(Namsan) — a different, easily-confused mountain name. Fix: KO "사천왕사를 낭산
  남쪽에 세웠다..." EN: "They built Sacheonwangsa to the south of Nangsan..."
- **P0010 n=3** — the audit proposes 仁容寺("Inyongsa")→仁問寺("Inmunsa") as a scribal
  correction, but 容 and 問 aren't an obvious copying-error pair, and I have no
  independent evidence this is corrupted rather than a distinct (if punning) temple
  name. Default to the hanmun as given: keep "Inyongsa," don't emend to "Inmunsa."
- **P0013 n2** — 塚宰 is a classical Chinese administrative title (from the Rites of
  Zhou — roughly "Chief Minister," head of state administration), not a religious
  office. Both the current text ("Grand Priest") and the audit's fix ("Grand Ritualist")
  keep the wrong religious framing — the job described ("govern all officials, pacify
  the realm") is plainly administrative. Fix: KO "그대는 총재(재상)를 맡아라." EN: "You
  shall take on the role of Chief Minister."
