# ch_14 — Claude review (5 equivalence + 18 quality = 23 items)

Biggest catch: **P0010 n4** — 竹旨 is literally the name "Jukji," the protagonist of this
entire story (죽지랑/Jukji-rang is named constantly throughout the chapter) — yet the
sentence that actually names him currently reads as if he was "named to follow the will
of death" and given no name at all, directly contradicting the rest of the chapter.

## ACCEPT (10)

- **P0002 n3, P0002 n4, P0003 n5, P0007 n4, P0008 n2, P0008 n5, P0009 n5** — real fixes,
  apply (mostly clause-level).
- **P0008 n4** (equiv/EXTRA = quality/TERM_ERROR, same tag) — 美郎之重士風味 means the
  youth *valued a scholar's dignity* (a positive trait) — current text inverts this into
  "looked down on Ikseon." fix correctly reframes it.
- **P0010 n2** (equiv/EXTRA = quality/TERM_ERROR, same tag) — 居士 = "hermit," not
  "layer." fix correct.
- **P0010 n5** (equiv/EXTRA = quality/TERM_ERROR, same tag) — hanmun names 4 reigns
  (真德/Jindeok, 太宗/Taejong, 文武/Munmu, 神文/Sinmun); current text merges the first two
  into one and invents a fabricated 4th ("Munmusa"). The audit's own fix correctly splits
  them into the right four names — use it as-is.

## REVISE (6)

- **P0003 n2** — the flagged issue (bamboo phrasing) is minor; the real error in the same
  sentence is 奏 ("reported [to the king]") mistranslated as "played [it]" — you can't
  "play" a mountain/bamboo phenomenon. Fix: KO "...사신이 와서 그것을 보고했다." EN:
  "...The envoy came and reported it to the king."
- **P0005 n5** — 代 ("during the reign of") got fused into the era name, producing
  "Daicheonsusa" for what should just be the 天授 (Cheonsu) era — and the audit's own
  fix keeps the same fusion. Fix: KO "천수 사년 계사년" (drop "대"), EN "the fourth year
  of the Cheonsu era" (drop "Dai").
- **P0007 n2** (equiv/SHIFTED+OMITTED, same tag) — bigger than the audit's own fix
  realizes: 幢典牟梁益宣阿干 parses as one person — "Ikseon, the standard-bearer officer
  (幢典) of Moryang (牟梁), rank Agan" — not two people, "Dangjeon and Ikseon." The
  audit's fix keeps both as separate names. Fix: KO "당전(지휘관)인 모량 출신 익선
  아간이 내 아들을 부산성 창고로 데려가려 합니다..." EN: "Ikseon, an Agan and the
  standard-bearer officer from Moryang, is taking my son to the Busanseong warehouse..."
- **P0009 n3** — 珍 here likely continues 侃珍 (Ganjin), the corrupt official named two
  sentences earlier, not a stray adjective "precious." Reading "史上侃珍子孫" as "Ganjin's
  descendants" fits the king's punitive decree far better than "families with precious
  descendants." Fix: KO "...대왕이 사관들에게 엄히 명하여 간진의 자손을 특별히 표시하여
  구별하게 했다." EN: "...the Great King strictly ordered the historians to mark
  Ganjin's descendants and set them apart."
- **P0010 n4** — see chapter note above. Fix: KO "아이가 태어나자 이름을 죽지라
  하였다." EN: "When the child was born, he was named Jukji." (not "Juk-ui" — 旨 reads
  "ji," matching "Jukji-rang" as used everywhere else in this chapter.)
- **P0013 n3** — 王為太宗大王刱奉德寺 means "the king built Bongdeoksa **for/in honor of**
  King Taejong" (為 = "for the sake of," a common classical usage) — not "the king
  became the Great King." Current text drops the dedication to Taejong entirely, and the
  audit's fix keeps the same misreading ("ascended as the Great King"). Fix: KO "성덕왕이
  태종대왕을 위하여 봉덕사를 지었다." EN: "King Seongdeok built Bongdeoksa in honor of
  King Taejong."

## REJECT (2)

- **P0004 n2** — "Yusin"→"Yushin" would be an internal-consistency *regression*: every
  other chapter in this corpus uses "Yusin" for 庾信. Keep "Yusin."
- **P0004 n4** — the note itself admits the flagged detail (十七日, seventeen days) is
  "correctly translated" already; no actionable issue.

Total: ACCEPT 13 (10 + 3 combined equiv/quality groups), REVISE 6, REJECT 2. (23 items.)
