# ch_22 — Claude review (7 equivalence + 35 quality = 42 items)

Biggest catch this chapter, missed entirely by both audit passes: **P0012 n2** — "遂命
誅之" ("[Taejo] then ordered him executed") refers to **Neunghwan**, the person being
interrogated in the immediately preceding sentence, not Singeom. The very next clause
explicitly says Singeom was "specially spared from death" (特原其死) because he'd been
coerced and had submitted himself. Current text (and the audit's fix) both have
"Gyeonhwon eventually executed Singeom" — attributing the execution to the wrong person
and directly contradicting the sentence three words later that says he was pardoned.
