## REVISE (2)

- **P0003 n3** — 三美 here means "three fortunate/praiseworthy outcomes," not "three
  beauties" — confirmed by P0004, which enumerates exactly three concrete benefits
  (ascending to the position, gaining access to something previously unavailable, and
  the brother/wife's joy), none of which are aesthetic. Both current text and the
  audit's fix keep "three beauties." Fix: KO "세 가지 아름다움"→"세 가지 좋은 일," EN
  "three beauties"→"three fortunate outcomes."
- **P0008 n3** — 勝事 more likely means "a meritorious deed" (i.e., building a temple,
  which is exactly what the very next sentence has the king order), not a person's title
  "Seungsa" being commanded to act — no such office is referenced anywhere else in the
  passage. Fix: KO "일관이 아뢰었다. '이는 동해 용이 조화를 부린 것이니, 마땅히 공덕이
  될 만한 일을 행하여 풀어야 합니다.' 미처 다 이르기도 전에 구름이 걷히고 안개가
  사라졌다." EN: "The Ilgwan said, 'This is the East Sea Dragon's doing; a meritorious
  deed should be performed to resolve it.' Before the order could even be carried out,
  the clouds dispersed and the mist vanished."
