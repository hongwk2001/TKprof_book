## Book Description / Blurb
**A sweeping, satirical masterpiece about the illusion of wealth and the rise of modern capitalism—now adapted for today's reader.**

Before the dot-com bubble and modern corporate scandals, there was *Tono-Bungay*. In one of H.G. Wells’ most ambitious and entertaining novels, young George Ponderevo is swept into a whirlwind of sudden, astronomical wealth when his eccentric uncle Edward invents a mildly stimulating, utterly useless patent medicine called "Tono-Bungay."

What begins as a small-town pharmacy scheme explodes into a massive, hollow empire of marketing, high society, and corporate trickery. As George rides the wave of his uncle's fraudulent fortune, he must grapple with the moral decay of Edwardian England, the pursuit of scientific truth through early aviation, and the realization that their entire empire is built on nothing but a catchy name and public gullibility.

Part social satire, part coming-of-age story, and part science fiction, *Tono-Bungay* is a brilliant critique of society's obsession with advertising and unearned wealth. 

Adapted for contemporary readers, this modernized edition retains the wit, scale, and brilliance of Wells' original prose while carefully updating archaic phrasing for effortless reading.
