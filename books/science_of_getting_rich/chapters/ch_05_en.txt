[Chapter 5. Increasing Life.]

You need to completely let go of the old idea that there's a God who wants you to be poor, or that your poverty somehow serves His plans.

The Intelligent Substance, which is everything and in everything, and which lives in everything and lives in you, is a consciously Living Substance. Since it's a consciously living substance, it naturally has the same inherent desire as every living intelligence: to increase life. Every living thing constantly seeks to expand its life, because life, just by existing, has to grow.

Think of a seed dropped into the ground. It becomes active, and just by living, it produces a hundred more seeds. Life multiplies itself simply by living. It is always Becoming More. It has to do this if it wants to continue existing at all.

Intelligence operates under this same need for constant growth. Every thought we think makes us need to think another thought; our consciousness is always expanding. Every fact we learn leads us to learn another fact; our knowledge is always increasing. Every talent we develop makes us want to develop another one. We are driven by life's urge to express itself, which constantly pushes us to know more, to do more, and to be more.

To know more, do more, and be more, we must have more. We need things to use, because we only learn, do, and become by using things. We must get rich so we can live more fully.

The desire for wealth is simply our capacity for a larger life trying to fulfill itself. Every desire is an unexpressed possibility trying to come into action. It's power trying to manifest that causes desire. The thing that makes you want more money is the same thing that makes a plant grow: it's Life, seeking fuller expression.

The One Living Substance must follow this inherent law of all life. It is filled with the desire to live more. That's why it has to create things.

The One Substance wants to live more through you. Therefore, it wants you to have all the things you can use.

It is God's desire that you become rich. He wants you to get rich because He can express Himself better through you if you have plenty of things to use to give Him expression. He can live more through you if you have unlimited access to the means of life.

The universe wants you to have everything you desire.

Nature supports your plans.

Everything is naturally on your side.

Decide that this is true.

It is crucial, however, that **your purpose aligns with the purpose that is in All**.

You must want real life, not just simple pleasure or physical indulgence. Life is about performing your functions. An individual truly lives only when they perform every function they're capable of—physical, mental, and spiritual—without going to extremes in any area.

You don't want to get rich just to live like an animal, satisfying only your base desires; that's not what life is about. However, performing every physical function is part of life. No one lives completely if they deny their body's impulses a normal and healthy expression.

You don't want to get rich just to enjoy mental pleasures, gain knowledge, satisfy ambition, outshine others, or become famous. All of these are legitimate parts of life. But someone who lives only for intellectual pleasures will have only a partial life, and they will never be satisfied with their situation.

You don't want to get rich only for the good of others, to sacrifice yourself for humanity's salvation, or to experience the joys of charity and self-sacrifice. The joys of the soul are only one part of life. They are no better or nobler than any other part.

You want to get rich so you can eat, drink, and be happy when it's time for those things. You want to surround yourself with beautiful things, see distant lands, nourish your mind, and develop your intellect. You want to be able to love people, do kind things, and play a good role in helping the world find truth.

But remember that extreme selflessness is no better or nobler than extreme selfishness. Both are mistakes.

Let go of the idea that God wants you to sacrifice yourself for others, or that you can earn His favor by doing so. God doesn't require anything like that.

What He wants is for you to make the most of yourself, for yourself and for others. And **you can help others more by making the most of yourself than in any other way**.

You can make the most of yourself only by getting rich. So, it's right and commendable that you should give your first and best attention to the work of acquiring wealth.

Remember, though, that the Substance desires good for everyone. Its actions must lead to more life for all. It cannot be made to work for less life for anyone, because it is equally present in everyone, seeking wealth and life.

Intelligent Substance will create things for you, but it won't take things away from someone else and give them to you.

You must get rid of any thoughts of competition. Your job is to create, not to compete for what already exists.

You don't have to take anything away from anyone.

You don't have to drive hard bargains.

You don't have to cheat or take advantage of anyone. You don't need to let anyone work for you for less than they earn.

You don't need to want what other people own, or look at their things with longing. No one has anything that you can't also have. And you can get it without taking anything away from them.

You are meant to be a creator, not a competitor. You will get what you want. But you'll do it in a way that ensures everyone else will have more than they do now.

I know that some people get a lot of money by doing the exact opposite of what I just said. Let me explain this a bit. Super-rich people sometimes get wealthy purely because of their amazing competitive skills. Other times, they unconsciously connect with the 'Substance' – the raw material of everything – and its big goals. This helps with the overall progress of humanity through industrial development. People like Rockefeller, Carnegie, and Morgan, among others, have been unconscious tools of the Supreme power. They did the necessary work of organizing and streamlining productive industries. Ultimately, their work will greatly help everyone live a better life. But their time is almost up. They've organized production. Soon, people representing the general public will take over and organize how things are distributed.

These multi-millionaires are like the giant reptiles from prehistoric times. They play a necessary role in evolution. But the same Power that created them will also get rid of them. It's also good to remember that they've never been truly rich. If you look at the private lives of most people in this group, you'll see they've actually been among the most miserable and unhappy of the poor.

Wealth gained through competition is never satisfying or lasting. It's yours today, and someone else's tomorrow. Remember this: if you want to get rich in a scientific and 'Certain Way,' you must completely stop thinking competitively. You must never believe, even for a second, that the supply is limited. The moment you start thinking that bankers and others are 'cornering' and controlling all the money, or that you need to push for laws to stop this, you fall into a competitive mindset. At that point, your power to create is temporarily gone. What's worse, you'll probably stop any creative progress you've already started.

Understand this: there are countless millions of dollars' worth of gold in the earth's mountains, still undiscovered. And even if there weren't, more would be created from 'Thinking Substance' to meet your needs.

Understand that the money you need will come. This is true even if it means a thousand people have to be led to discover new gold mines tomorrow.

Never focus on what's visibly available. Instead, always look at the limitless wealth in 'Formless Substance.' And know that it's coming to you as quickly as you can accept and use it. No one can stop you from getting what's yours by hoarding the visible supply.

So, never let yourself think, even for a second, that all the best building spots will be taken before you're ready to build your house, forcing you to rush. Don't ever worry about big corporations and monopolies, or get anxious that they'll soon own the entire planet. Never be afraid that you'll lose what you want because someone else 'gets it first.' That simply can't happen. You aren't trying to get something someone else already has. You are causing what you want to be created from 'Formless Substance,' and there's an unlimited supply. Stick to this core idea:

There is a 'thinking stuff' from which everything is made. In its original form, it spreads through, goes into, and fills all the empty spaces of the universe.

A thought, within this 'substance,' creates the very thing that the thought pictures.

People can form things in their minds. By impressing their thoughts onto 'formless substance,' they can cause the thing they're thinking about to be created.
