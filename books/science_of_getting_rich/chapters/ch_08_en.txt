[Chapter 8: Thinking in the Certain Way]

Go back to Chapter 6 and read the story again about the man who pictured his house in his mind. That will give you a good idea of the first step to getting rich. You need to create a clear, specific mental picture of what you want. You can't send out an idea if you don't have it clearly in your own mind first.

You have to have that clear picture before you can project it. Many people fail to make an impression on Thinking Substance because their own ideas are vague and unclear about what they want to do, have, or become.

It's not enough to just have a general desire for wealth "to do good with." Everyone has that desire.

It's also not enough to just wish to travel, see things, or live more. Everyone has those desires too. If you were sending a text message to a friend, you wouldn't just send them the alphabet in order and expect them to figure out the message. You also wouldn't just pick random words from a dictionary. You would send a clear, meaningful sentence. When you try to impress your desires upon Substance, remember that you need to do it with a clear, coherent statement. You must know exactly what you want, and be specific.

You can never get rich, or activate the creative power, by sending out unclear longings and vague desires.

Review your desires just like the man I described reviewed his house. See exactly what you want, and create a clear mental picture of how you want it to look when you get it.

You must keep that clear mental picture in your mind constantly, just like a sailor keeps the destination port in mind while steering the ship. You need to always keep your focus on it. You must not lose sight of it any more than a helmsman loses sight of the compass.

You don't need to do concentration exercises, set aside special times for prayer and affirmations, "go into the silence," or perform any kind of mystical tricks. These things are fine, but all you really need is to know what you want, and to want it so strongly that it stays in your thoughts.

Spend as much of your free time as you can thinking about your mental picture. But nobody needs to do exercises to concentrate their mind on something they truly want. It's the things you don't really care about that require effort to focus your attention on.

And unless you truly want to get rich, with a desire strong enough to keep your thoughts focused on that goal, just like a magnetic pole holds a compass needle, it will barely be worth your time to try and follow the instructions in this book.

The methods explained here are for people whose desire for wealth is strong enough to overcome mental laziness and the love of comfort, and make them take action.

So, the clearer and more specific you make your mental picture, and the more you focus on it, bringing out all its delightful details, the stronger your desire will become. And the stronger your desire, the easier it will be to keep your mind fixed on the picture of what you want.

However, something more is necessary than just seeing the picture clearly. If that's all you do, you're just a dreamer, and you'll have little to no power to achieve anything.

Behind your clear vision, there must be the purpose to make it real, to bring it into tangible existence.

And behind this purpose, there must be an invincible and unwavering FAITH that the thing is already yours. Believe that it is "at hand," and you just need to take possession of it.

Mentally live in the new house until it physically appears around you. In your mind, immediately start fully enjoying the things you want.

Jesus said, "Whatever you ask for when you pray, believe that you have received them, and they will be yours."

Picture the things you want as if they are actually around you all the time. See yourself owning and using them. Use them in your imagination just as you will use them when they become your real possessions. Focus on your mental picture until it's clear and distinct. Then, adopt the Mental Attitude of Ownership toward everything in that picture. Mentally take possession of it, with complete faith that it is truly yours. Maintain this mental ownership. Do not waver for even a second in your faith that it is real.

And remember what was said in an earlier chapter about gratitude. Be as thankful for it all the time as you expect to be when it has physically appeared. The person who can sincerely thank God for things they only own in their imagination right now has real faith. They will get rich. They will cause the creation of whatever they want.

You don't need to pray repeatedly for the things you want. It's not necessary to tell God about it every day.

Jesus told His students, "Don't use meaningless repetitions like non-believers do, because your Father knows what you need before you even ask Him."

Your role is to intelligently formulate your desires for the things that lead to a bigger life. Then, arrange these desires into a coherent whole. After that, impress this complete Desire upon the Formless Substance, which has the power and the will to bring you what you want.

You don't make this impression by repeating strings of words. You make it by holding the vision with an unshakable PURPOSE to achieve it, and with steadfast FAITH that you *will* achieve it.

The answer to prayer isn't based on your faith while you're talking, but on your faith while you're working.

You can't impress the mind of God by setting aside a special Sabbath day to tell Him what you want, and then forgetting about Him for the rest of the week. You also can't impress Him by having special hours to go into your private space and pray, only to dismiss the matter from your mind until the next prayer time.

Speaking your prayers aloud is fine, and it has an effect, especially on you. It helps clarify your vision and strengthens your faith. But it's not just your spoken requests that get you what you want. To get rich, you don't need a "sweet hour of prayer." You need to "pray without ceasing." And by "prayer," I mean consistently holding onto your vision, with the purpose of bringing it into solid form, and the faith that you are actively doing so.

"Believe that you *receive* them."

The entire process hinges on receiving, once you have clearly formed your vision. Once you *have* formed it, it's good to make a spoken statement, addressing the Supreme in reverent prayer. And from that moment, you must, in your mind, receive what you've asked for. Live in the new house. Wear the fine clothes. Ride in the car. Go on the trip, and confidently plan for even bigger trips. Think and speak of all the things you've asked for as if you actually own them right now. Imagine an environment and a financial situation exactly as you want them. Then, live all the time in that imaginary environment and financial situation. Be careful, though, not to do this as just a dreamer or someone building castles in the air. Hold onto the FAITH that what you imagine is becoming real, and hold onto the PURPOSE to make it real. Remember that it's faith and purpose in using your imagination that separates the scientist from the dreamer. Once you've learned this fact, this is where you must learn the proper use of your Will.
