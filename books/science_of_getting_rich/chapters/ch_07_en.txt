Chapter 7. Gratitude

The examples from the last chapter should have shown you that the first step toward getting rich is to communicate your desires to the Formless Substance.

This is true. You'll see that to do this, you need to connect with the Formless Intelligence in a harmonious way.

Achieving this harmonious connection is incredibly important. So, I'll spend some time discussing it here. I'll give you instructions that, if you follow them, will definitely bring your mind into perfect unity with God.

The entire process of mental adjustment and becoming one with God can be summed up in one word: gratitude.

First, you believe there's one Intelligent Substance that everything comes from. Second, you believe this Substance gives you everything you desire. And third, you connect with It through a feeling of deep and profound gratitude.

Many people live their lives correctly in every other way, but they stay poor because they lack gratitude. After receiving one gift from God, they cut the connection to Him by not acknowledging it.

It's easy to understand that the closer we live to the source of wealth, the more wealth we'll receive. It's also easy to see that a soul always filled with gratitude lives in closer touch with God than one that never thankfully acknowledges Him.

The more gratefully we focus our minds on the Supreme when good things happen, the more good things we will receive. They will also come more quickly. The simple reason is that a mental attitude of gratitude brings your mind into closer contact with the source of those blessings.

Perhaps it's a new idea for you that gratitude brings your entire mind into closer harmony with the universe's creative energies. If so, think about it carefully, and you'll see it's true. The good things you already have came to you because you followed certain laws. Gratitude will guide your mind along the paths where things come from. It will also keep you in close harmony with creative thought and stop you from falling into competitive thought.

Only gratitude can keep you focused on the All. It prevents you from making the mistake of thinking the supply is limited. Believing that would be fatal to your hopes.

There is a Law of Gratitude. It's absolutely necessary that you follow this law if you want to get the results you're looking for.

The law of gratitude is the natural principle that action and reaction are always equal and move in opposite directions.

When your mind gratefully reaches out in thankful praise to the Supreme, it's like releasing or spending energy. This energy will definitely reach its target. And the reaction is an instant movement toward you.

“Come close to God, and He will come close to you.” That's a statement of psychological truth.

If your gratitude is strong and constant, the reaction in Formless Substance will be strong and continuous. The things you want will always move toward you. Notice the grateful attitude Jesus had. He always seemed to be saying, “Father, I thank You that You hear me.” You can't use much power without gratitude, because gratitude is what keeps you connected to Power.

But the value of gratitude isn't just about getting you more blessings in the future. Without gratitude, you can't avoid feeling dissatisfied with how things are for very long.

The moment you let your mind dwell on dissatisfaction with how things are, you start to lose progress. You focus your attention on what's common, ordinary, poor, squalid, and mean. Then, your mind takes on the form of these things. You'll transmit these forms, or mental images, to the Formless, and the common, poor, squalid, and mean will come to you.

If you let your mind dwell on inferior things, you'll become inferior and surround yourself with inferior things.

On the other hand, if you focus your attention on the best, you'll surround yourself with the best and become the best.

The Creative Power within us shapes us into the image of whatever we focus our attention on.

We are Thinking Substance. Thinking substance always takes the form of whatever it thinks about.

A grateful mind is constantly focused on the best. Therefore, it tends to become the best. It takes on the form or character of the best, and it will receive the best.

Also, faith comes from gratitude. A grateful mind constantly expects good things, and that expectation turns into faith. The way gratitude affects your own mind produces faith. Every wave of thankful thanksgiving you send out increases faith. Someone who feels no gratitude can't keep a living faith for long. And without a living faith, you can't get rich using the creative method, as we'll see in the following chapters.

So, it's necessary to develop the habit of being grateful for every good thing that comes your way. And you should give thanks continuously.

Since all things have contributed to your progress, you should include everything in your gratitude.

Don't waste time thinking or talking about the flaws or wrong actions of wealthy elites or powerful business leaders. Their organization of the world has created your opportunities. Everything you get truly comes to you because of them.

Don't rage against corrupt politicians. If it weren't for politicians, we'd fall into anarchy, and your opportunities would be greatly reduced.

God has worked for a long time and very patiently to bring us to where we are in industry and government. He's going to keep right on with His work. There's no doubt at all that He will get rid of wealthy elites, powerful business leaders, industry giants, and politicians as soon as they're no longer needed. But for now, understand that they are all very good. Remember that they are all helping to set up the channels through which your riches will come to you. Be grateful to all of them. This will bring you into harmonious relationships with the good in everything, and the good in everything will move toward you.
