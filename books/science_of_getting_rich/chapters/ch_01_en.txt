Preface

This book is practical, not philosophical. It's a hands-on guide, not a deep dive into theories. It's for people whose most urgent need is money. They want to get rich first, and then they can think about philosophy later. It's also for those who haven't had the time, resources, or chance to study metaphysics in depth. But they still want results. They're willing to accept scientific conclusions as a basis for action, without needing to understand all the steps that led to those conclusions.

We expect readers to accept the basic principles on faith. Think of it like accepting a law of electricity from someone like Marconi or Edison. Once you accept these principles on faith, you'll prove they're true by acting on them without fear or hesitation. Anyone who does this will definitely get rich. That's because the science used here is exact, and failure is impossible. However, for those who want to explore the philosophical theories and find a logical reason for their faith, I'll mention some key sources here.

The monistic theory of the universe comes from Hindu origins. This theory states that everything is one, and that one Substance appears in many forms as the different elements of the material world. It has slowly gained acceptance in Western thought over the past two hundred years. It forms the basis of all Eastern philosophies, as well as the ideas of thinkers like Descartes, Spinoza, Leibnitz, Schopenhauer, Hegel, and Emerson.

If you want to dig into the philosophical foundations, we recommend reading Hegel and Emerson. You should also check out "The Eternal News," an excellent pamphlet published by J. J. Brown, located at 300 Cathcart Road, Govanhill, Glasgow, Scotland. You might also find help in a series of articles I wrote. They were published in Nautilus (Holyoke, Mass.) during the spring and summer of 1909, under the title "What is Truth?"

When I wrote this book, I prioritized clear and simple language above everything else, so that everyone could understand it. The action plan presented here comes from philosophical conclusions. It has been thoroughly tested and has passed the ultimate test of practical experience: it works. If you want to know how these conclusions were reached, read the works of the authors I mentioned earlier. But if you want to see the practical benefits of their philosophies in your own life, then read this book and do exactly what it tells you.

Wallace D. Wattles
