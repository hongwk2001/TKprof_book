Chapter 15
The Advancing Person

What I've said in the last chapter applies just as much to professionals and wage-earners as it does to people in business.

Whether you're a doctor, a teacher, or a religious leader, if you can give others an increase of life and make them realize it, they will be drawn to you, and you will get rich. A doctor who holds the vision of themselves as a great and successful healer, and who works toward fully realizing that vision with faith and purpose (as described in previous chapters), will connect so closely with the Source of Life that they will become phenomenally successful. Patients will come to them in crowds.

No one has a greater opportunity to put the teachings of this book into practice than someone in the medical field. It doesn't matter what specific school of medicine they belong to, because the principle of healing is universal and accessible to all of them. The Advancing Person in medicine, who holds a clear mental picture of themselves as successful and who follows the laws of faith, purpose, and gratitude, will cure every curable case they take on, regardless of the specific treatments they use.

In the field of religion, the world is crying out for leaders who can teach their congregations the true science of abundant life. Anyone who masters the details of the science of getting rich—along with the related sciences of being healthy, being great, and winning love—and who teaches these principles from the pulpit, will never lack an audience. This is the good news the world needs. It provides an increase of life, and people will listen to it gladly and generously support the person who brings it to them.

What we need now is a demonstration of the science of life from our religious leaders. We need preachers who don't just tell us how, but who show us how through their own lives. We need leaders who are themselves rich, healthy, great, and loved, to teach us how to achieve those same things. When someone like that steps up, they will find a massive and loyal following.

The same is true for teachers who can inspire children with the faith and purpose of an advancing life. They will never be out of a job. And any teacher who possesses this faith and purpose can pass it on to their students. They can't help but pass it on if it's a genuine part of their own life and practice.

What's true for the teacher, preacher, and doctor is true for the lawyer, dentist, real estate agent, insurance broker—for absolutely everyone.

The combination of mental and personal action I've described is foolproof. It simply cannot fail. Every person who follows these instructions consistently, persistently, and to the letter, will get rich. The law of the Increase of Life is as mathematically certain in how it works as the law of gravity. Getting rich is an exact science.

Wage-earners will find this is just as true for them as for anyone else mentioned. Don't feel like you have no chance to get rich just because you're working somewhere with no visible opportunity for advancement, where the pay is low and the cost of living is high. Form a clear mental vision of what you want, and start acting with faith and purpose.

Do all the work you can do every single day, and do every piece of that work perfectly. Put the power of success and the purpose to get rich into everything you do.

But don't do this just to suck up to your boss, hoping that they, or someone higher up, will notice your good work and promote you. It's unlikely they will.

A person who is merely a "good" worker, doing their current job to the very best of their ability and feeling satisfied with that, is very valuable to their employer. It's usually not in the employer's interest to promote them; they are worth more exactly where they are.

To actually secure advancement, you need more than just being too big for your current role.

The person who is guaranteed to advance is the one who is too big for their place, who has a clear concept of what they want to be, who knows they can become what they want to be, and who is determined to BE what they want to be.

Don't try to over-deliver in your current job just to please your employer. Do it with the intention of advancing yourself. Hold onto the faith and purpose of increase during work hours, after work hours, and before work hours. Hold it in such a way that everyone who interacts with you—whether it's a supervisor, a coworker, or a friend—feels the power of your purpose radiating from you. Make sure everyone gets a sense of advancement and increase from you. People will be drawn to you. And if there's no possibility for advancement in your current job, you'll very quickly see an opportunity to take another one.

There is a Power that never fails to present opportunities to the Advancing Person who is operating in alignment with these laws.

The Supreme Power can't help but help you if you act in a Certain Way. It has to do so in order to help Itself.

There is nothing in your circumstances or in the broader economy that can keep you down. If you can't get rich working for a massive corporation, you can get rich on a small farm. And if you start moving in the Certain Way, you will definitely escape the grip of that corporation and make it to the farm, or wherever else you want to be.

If even a few thousand employees started acting in the Certain Way, the massive corporations would quickly be in trouble. They would either have to give their workers more opportunities, or go out of business. Nobody is forced to work for a mega-corporation. These companies can only keep people in seemingly hopeless conditions as long as those people are either unaware of the science of getting rich, or too intellectually lazy to practice it.

Start this way of thinking and acting, and your faith and purpose will make you quick to spot any opportunity to improve your situation.

Those opportunities will come quickly, because the Supreme Power, working in everything and working for you, will bring them right to you.

Don't wait for an opportunity that gives you everything you want to be all at once. When an opportunity to be more than you are now presents itself, and you feel pulled toward it, take it. It will be the first step toward an even greater opportunity.

In this universe, there is no such thing as a lack of opportunity for someone who is living an advancing life.

It is built into the very fabric of the cosmos that all things will work for them and work together for their good. They must certainly get rich if they act and think in the Certain Way. So, wage-earners everywhere should study this book carefully and confidently start taking the actions it prescribes. It will not fail.
