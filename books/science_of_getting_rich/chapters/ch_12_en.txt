Chapter 12
Efficient Action

You must direct your thoughts as described in previous chapters, and begin to do what you can, right where you are. And you must do EVERYTHING that you can do in your current situation.

You can only advance by outgrowing your present place. And you haven't outgrown your present place if you leave any of the work connected to it unfinished.

The world only moves forward because of people who do more than just fill their present roles.

If no one fully completed their current duties, everything would start moving backward. Those who don't quite fill their present places are a dead weight on society, government, commerce, and industry. They have to be carried along by others at a great expense. The progress of the world is only held back by those who don't fill the spaces they occupy. They belong to a past era and a lower stage of life, and their tendency is toward decline. No society could advance if everyone was smaller than their role. Social evolution is guided by the same laws as physical and mental evolution. In the animal world, evolution is driven by an excess of life.

When an organism has more life than it can express through its current functions, it develops organs for a higher level of existence, and a new species is born.

There would never have been new species if there weren't organisms that more than filled their places. The law is exactly the same for you. Getting rich depends on you applying this principle to your own life.

Every day is either a success or a failure, and it's the successful days that get you what you want. If every day is a failure, you can never get rich. But if every day is a success, you cannot fail to get rich.

If there's something that could be done today, and you don't do it, you have failed as far as that specific thing is concerned. And the consequences might be more disastrous than you imagine.

You can't foresee the results of even the most trivial act. You don't know how all the forces that have been set in motion on your behalf are working. A lot might depend on you doing some simple act. It might be the very thing that opens the door to massive opportunities. You can never know all the combinations the Supreme Intelligence is putting together for you in the physical world and in human affairs. Your neglect or failure to do some small thing might cause a long delay in getting what you want.

Do ALL that can be done each day.

There is, however, a limit or condition to this that you must consider.

You shouldn't overwork, and you shouldn't blindly rush into your business trying to do the greatest possible number of things in the shortest amount of time.

Don't try to do tomorrow's work today, and don't try to cram a week's work into a single day.

It's really not the number of things you do, but the EFFICIENCY of each separate action that counts.

Every act is, in itself, either a success or a failure.

Every act is, in itself, either effective or ineffective.

Every inefficient act is a failure, and if you spend your life doing inefficient things, your whole life will be a failure.

The more things you do, the worse it is for you, if all your acts are inefficient.

On the other hand, every efficient act is a success in itself. And if every act of your life is efficient, your whole life MUST be a success.

The cause of failure is doing too many things inefficiently, and not doing enough things efficiently.

You can see it's a self-evident truth that if you don't do any inefficient acts, and if you do a sufficient number of efficient ones, you will become rich. Now, if it's possible for you to make each act efficient, you can see again that getting rich is reduced to an exact science, like mathematics.

So, the whole thing hinges on whether you can make each separate act a success in itself. And you most certainly can.

You can make each act a success because All Power is working with you, and All Power cannot fail.

Power is at your service. To make each act efficient, you simply have to put power into it.

Every action is either strong or weak. When every action is strong, you are acting in the Certain Way that will make you rich.

Every act can be made strong and efficient by holding onto your vision while you are doing it, and putting the full power of your FAITH and PURPOSE into it.

This is exactly where people fail when they separate mental power from personal action. They use the power of their mind in one place and at one time, but they act in another place and at another time. As a result, their acts aren't successful in themselves; too many of them are inefficient. But if All Power goes into every act, no matter how ordinary, every act will be a success in itself. And since every success naturally opens the way to other successes, your progress toward what you want—and the progress of what you want toward you—will become increasingly rapid.

Remember that successful action builds upon itself. Because the desire for more life is built into everything, when a person starts moving toward a larger life, more things attach themselves to them, and the influence of their desire multiplies.

Do all that you can do every day, and do each act efficiently.

When I say you must hold onto your vision while doing every act, no matter how trivial or ordinary, I don't mean you need to see the vision clearly down to its smallest details at all times. It should be the work of your free time to use your imagination to focus on the details of your vision, contemplating them until they are firmly fixed in your memory.

If you want fast results, spend practically all your spare time doing this.

Through continuous contemplation, you will get the picture of what you want, even down to the smallest details, so firmly fixed in your mind—and so completely transferred to the mind of the Formless Substance—that during your working hours, you only need to mentally refer to the picture to boost your faith and purpose, and bring out your best effort. Contemplate your picture during your free time until your consciousness is so full of it that you can grasp it instantly. You will become so excited by its bright promises that just thinking about it will draw out the strongest energies of your whole being.

Let's repeat our core principles again, slightly changing the closing statements to reflect the point we've now reached.

There is a thinking substance from which all things are made. In its original state, it permeates, penetrates, and fills the spaces of the universe.

A thought within this substance produces the very thing that the thought imagines.

A person can form things in their mind, and, by impressing those thoughts upon the formless substance, can cause the thing they think about to be created.

To do this, a person must shift from a competitive mindset to a creative one. They must form a clear mental picture of what they want, and do, with faith and purpose, all that can be done each day, doing each separate thing efficiently.
