Thought is the only power that can produce real riches from the Formless Substance. Everything is made from a substance that thinks. When this substance thinks of a form, it creates that form.

The Original Substance moves based on its thoughts. Every shape and process you see in nature is a visible expression of a thought within the Original Substance. We live in a world of thought, which is part of a thought universe.

When creating, the Formless seems to follow the patterns of motion it has already set up. For example, thinking of an oak tree doesn't instantly create a full-grown tree. Instead, it starts the forces that will produce the tree, following those established growth patterns.

Every thought of a form, when held in Thinking Substance, causes that form to be created. However, this usually happens along existing patterns of growth and action.

If you impress the thought of a specific house design onto Formless Substance, it might not instantly create the house. Instead, it would redirect creative energies already active in trade and commerce. These energies would then quickly lead to the house being built. But if there were no existing ways for that creative energy to work, then the house would be formed directly from the primal substance. It wouldn't have to wait for the slow processes of the living and non-living world.

*No thought of a form can be impressed upon Original Substance without causing that form to be created.*

*There is a Thinking Stuff from which all things are made. In its original state, it permeates, penetrates, and fills all the empty spaces in the universe.*
