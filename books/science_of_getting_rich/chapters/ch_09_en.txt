[Chapter 9. How to Use Your Will]

If you want to get rich using a scientific method, you shouldn't try to use your willpower on anything outside of yourself.

You don't have the right to do that, anyway.

It's wrong to use your will on other people to make them do what you want.

It's just as obviously wrong to force people to do things with your mind as it is to force them physically. If you physically force people to do things for you, it turns them into slaves. Forcing them mentally does the exact same thing. The only difference is the method. If taking things from people by physical force is robbery, then taking things by mental force is also robbery. There's no difference in the basic principle.

You have no right to use your willpower on another person, even if you think it's "for their own good," because you don't actually know what's best for them.

The science of getting rich doesn't require you to use power or force on anyone else, in any way at all. There's absolutely no need to do that. In fact, trying to use your will on others will only work against your goal.

You don't need to use your will on things to force them to come to you.

That would just be trying to force God to do something, and that would be foolish, useless, and disrespectful.

You don't have to force God to give you good things, any more than you have to use your willpower to make the sun rise.

You don't have to use your willpower to conquer an unfriendly god, or to make stubborn, rebellious forces do what you want.

Substance is friendly towards you, and it's more eager to give you what you want than you are to get it.

To get rich, you only need to use your willpower on yourself.

Once you know what to think and do, you must use your will to make yourself think and do the right things. That's the proper way to use your will to get what you want: to keep yourself on the right path. Use your will to keep yourself thinking and acting in the Certain Way.

Don't try to project your will, your thoughts, or your mind out into space to "act" on things or people.

Keep your mind focused on yourself. It can achieve more there than anywhere else.

Use your mind to create a mental image of what you want, and hold onto that vision with faith and purpose. Then, use your will to keep your mind working in the Right Way.

The more steady and continuous your faith and purpose are, the faster you'll get rich. This is because you'll only make POSITIVE impressions on Substance, and you won't cancel them out with negative impressions.

The mental picture of what you desire, held with faith and purpose, is picked up by the Formless. It then spreads through it over vast distances—throughout the entire universe, as far as I know.

As this impression spreads, everything starts moving towards making it real. Every living thing, every inanimate object, and even things that don't exist yet are stirred into bringing what you want into being. All energy starts to be directed that way. Everything begins to move towards you. People's minds, everywhere, are influenced to do what's necessary to fulfill your desires. They work for you, without even knowing it.

But you can stop all this by creating a negative impression in the Formless Substance. Doubt or disbelief will just as surely push things away from you as faith and purpose will draw them towards you. Most people who try to use "mental science" to get rich fail because they don't understand this. Every hour and moment you spend dwelling on doubts and fears, every hour you spend worrying, every hour your mind is filled with disbelief—all of that creates a current that pushes things away from you throughout the entire realm of intelligent Substance. Remember this: All the promises are for those who believe, and only for them. Notice how strongly Jesus emphasized this point about belief. Now you know why.

Since belief is so important, you need to guard your thoughts. Your beliefs are largely shaped by what you observe and think about, so it's crucial that you control your attention.

And this is where your will comes in. It's through your will that you decide what to focus your attention on.

If you want to get rich, you absolutely must not study poverty.

Things don't come into existence by thinking about their opposites. You'll never achieve health by studying disease and thinking about it. You won't promote righteousness by studying sin and thinking about it. And no one ever got rich by studying poverty and thinking about poverty.

Medicine, as a science focused on disease, has actually increased disease. Religion, as a science focused on sin, has promoted sin. And economics, as a study of poverty, will only fill the world with misery and lack.

Don't talk about poverty. Don't investigate it or concern yourself with it. Never mind what causes it; you have nothing to do with those causes.

What concerns you is the cure.

Don't spend your time on charitable work or charity movements. All charity only tends to make the misery it tries to get rid of last longer.

I'm not saying you should be hard-hearted or unkind, or refuse to hear someone in need. But you must not try to get rid of poverty using any of the usual methods. Put poverty behind you, put everything related to it behind you, and "make good."

Get rich; that is the best way you can help the poor.

And you cannot keep the mental image that will make you rich if you fill your mind with pictures of poverty. Do not read books or papers that give detailed stories about how terrible life is for people in tenements, or about the horrors of child labor, and similar things. Do not read anything that fills your mind with depressing images of poverty and pain.

You cannot help the poor at all by knowing about these things. And just knowing about these problems doesn't help get rid of poverty at all.

What actually helps eliminate poverty isn't filling your mind with images of poverty. It's putting images of wealth into the minds of poor people.

You are not abandoning the poor in their suffering when you refuse to let your mind be filled with images of that suffering.

Poverty can be eliminated, not by increasing the number of wealthy people who think about poverty, but by increasing the number of poor people who decide with faith to get rich.

The poor do not need charity; they need inspiration. Charity only sends them a loaf of bread to keep them alive in their misery. Or it gives them entertainment to make them forget for an hour or two. But inspiration will help them rise out of their misery. If you want to help the poor, show them that they can become rich. Prove it by getting rich yourself.

The only way poverty will ever be removed from this world is by getting more and more people to practice what this book teaches.

People must be taught to become rich by creation, not by competition.

Every person who gets rich through competition kicks away the ladder they used to climb, holding others back. But every person who gets rich through creation opens a path for thousands to follow and inspires them to do it.

You are not showing a hard heart or an uncaring attitude when you refuse to feel sorry for poverty. You're not being mean when you refuse to see it, read about it, think or talk about it, or listen to others who do. Use your willpower to keep your mind OFF the topic of poverty. Instead, keep it focused with faith and purpose ON the vision of what you want.
