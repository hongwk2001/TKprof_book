[Chapter 6. How Riches Come to You]
When I say you don't have to negotiate aggressively, I don't mean you shouldn't make any deals at all. Nor do I mean you're above interacting with other people. What I mean is that you won't need to treat them unfairly. You don't have to get something for nothing. Instead, you can give every person more than you take from them.

You can't give every person more in market value than you take from them in cash. However, you can give them more in practical value than the cash value of what you take. For example, the paper, ink, and other materials in this book might not be worth the money you paid for it. But if the ideas it suggests bring you thousands of dollars, then the sellers haven't treated you unfairly. They've given you a lot of practical value for a small amount of cash.

Let's say I own a painting by a famous artist. In any civilized society, it would be worth thousands of dollars. I take it to Baffin Bay and, through "salesmanship," convince an Inuit person to give me a bundle of furs worth $500 for it. I've actually treated that person unfairly. They have no use for the painting; it has no practical value to them. It won't improve their life at all.

But what if I give him a gun worth $50 for his furs instead? Then he's gotten a good deal. He can use the gun. It will help him get many more furs and a lot of food. It will improve his life in every way. It will make him rich.

When you move from a competitive mindset to a creative one, you can examine your business deals very carefully. If you're selling someone something that doesn't add more to their life than what they give you in return, you should stop doing it. You don't have to take advantage of anyone in business. And if you're in a business that does take advantage of people, get out of it immediately.

Give every person more in practical value than you take from them in cash value. If you do this, you'll be adding to the world's life with every business transaction.

If you have people working for you, you must take more in cash value from them than you pay them in wages. However, you can organize your business in a way that it's full of the principle of advancement. This means every employee who wants to can advance a little bit every day.

You can make your business do for your employees what this book is doing for you. You can run your business so it becomes a kind of ladder. Every employee who makes the effort can climb that ladder to become wealthy themselves. If you give them the opportunity and they don't take it, it's not your fault.

Finally, you will create your wealth from the Formless Substance that is everywhere around you. But that doesn't mean your wealth will just appear out of thin air right in front of your eyes.

For example, if you want a sewing machine, I'm not telling you to imprint the idea of a sewing machine onto Thinking Substance until the machine forms by itself in the room where you're sitting, or somewhere else. But if you want a sewing machine, hold a mental image of it with absolute certainty that it's being made or is on its way to you. Once you've formed that thought, have complete and unwavering faith that the sewing machine is coming. Never think or speak of it as anything other than something that's definitely arriving. Claim it as already yours.

The power of the Supreme Intelligence will bring it to you, influencing people's minds. If you live in Maine, for instance, someone might be brought from Texas or Japan to get involved in a deal that will lead to you getting what you want.

If that happens, the whole situation will benefit that person just as much as it benefits you.

Don't forget for a moment that the Thinking Substance is everywhere, in everything, communicating with everything, and can influence everything. The desire of Thinking Substance for a fuller and better life has caused all the sewing machines already made to be created. It can cause millions more to be created, and it will, whenever people activate it through desire, faith, and by acting in a Certain Way.

You can certainly have a sewing machine in your house. And it's just as certain that you can have anything else you want, as long as you'll use it to improve your own life and the lives of others.

You don't need to hesitate about asking for a lot. Jesus said, "Your Father is pleased to give you the kingdom."

Original Substance wants to experience everything possible through you. It wants you to have everything you can or will use to live the most abundant life.

If you firmly grasp the idea that your desire for wealth is the same as God's desire for more complete expression, your faith becomes unbeatable.

Once, I saw a little boy sitting at a piano, trying in vain to make harmonious sounds from the keys. I could see he was upset and frustrated because he couldn't play real music. I asked him why he was so frustrated, and he answered, "I can feel the music in me, but I can't make my hands go right." The music in him was the URGE of Original Substance, holding all the possibilities of all life. All the potential for music was trying to express itself through the child.

God, the One Substance, is trying to live, do, and enjoy things through humanity. He is saying, "I want hands to build wonderful structures, to play beautiful music, to paint magnificent paintings. I want feet to carry out my tasks, eyes to see my beautiful creations, tongues to speak powerful truths and to sing wonderful songs," and so on.

All potential is seeking expression through people. God wants those who can play music to have pianos and every other instrument, and to have the resources to develop their talents completely. He wants those who can appreciate beauty to be able to surround themselves with beautiful things. He wants those who can understand truth to have every opportunity to travel and observe. He wants those who can appreciate clothing to be dressed beautifully, and those who can appreciate good food to be fed lavishly.

He wants all these things because it is He Himself who enjoys and appreciates them. It is God who wants to play, and sing, and enjoy beauty, and declare truth, and wear fine clothes, and eat good foods.

Paul said, "It is God who works in you to want and to do."

The desire you feel for wealth is the Infinite, seeking to express Himself in you just as He sought to find expression in the little boy at the piano.

So, you don't need to hesitate to ask for a lot.

Your part is to focus and express the desires of God.

This is a difficult point for most people. They still hold onto the old idea that poverty and self-sacrifice are what God wants. They see poverty as part of a divine plan, a natural necessity. They believe that God has finished His work and created everything He possibly can, and that most people must remain poor because there isn't enough to go around. They cling to this mistaken belief so strongly that they feel ashamed to ask for wealth. They try not to want more than a very modest income, just enough to make them reasonably comfortable.

I remember the case of one student who was told he must form a clear mental picture of the things he desired. This was so the creative thought of those things could be imprinted on Formless Substance. He was a very poor man, living in a rented house and living paycheck to paycheck. He couldn't truly believe that all wealth was available to him. So, after considering it carefully, he decided he might realistically ask for a new rug for his best room and a coal stove to heat the house during the cold weather. Following the instructions in this book, he got these things in a few months. Then it dawned on him that he hadn't asked for enough. He walked through the house he lived in and planned all the improvements he'd like to make. He mentally added a bay window in one spot and a room in another until it was complete in his mind as his dream home. Then he planned its furnishings.

Holding that whole picture in his mind, he began living in the Certain Way and working towards what he wanted. He owns the house now and is remodeling it to match his mental image. And now, with even greater faith, he's moving on to get bigger things. Things have happened for him according to his faith, and it's the same for you and for all of us.
