Introduction

Welcome to this modernized edition of *The Science of Getting Rich*, the classic wealth manual originally written by Wallace D. Wattles in 1910. 

For over a century, Wattles’ bold claim—that getting rich is an exact science, like algebra or arithmetic—has inspired countless readers, entrepreneurs, and thinkers. It served as the foundation for massive modern success movements, most notably inspiring the bestselling phenomenon *The Secret*. 

About This Modernized Edition

While the core principles of Wattles' philosophy remain timeless, the original 1910 text was written using somewhat archaic grammar, long, meandering sentence structures, and outdated vocabulary. 

In this edition, we have carefully modernized the prose to make it significantly more accessible for today's casual readers. 

Our modernization process focused on:
- Breaking down complex, overly long Victorian sentences into clear, punchy modern English.
- Removing obsolete pronouns and archaic formatting.
- Optimizing the flow of the text so it is perfect for both reading and listening via Text-to-Speech .

At the same time, we have preserved every single drop of Wattles' original wisdom. None of the actual meaning, details, or intensity of his argument have been omitted or summarized. We have also carefully retained the essential New Thought vocabulary and key concepts that form the backbone of the book, such as acting in the "Certain Way," connecting with the "Formless Substance," and cultivating a "creative mind" rather than a competitive one.

Prepare to shift your mindset, leave competition behind, and embrace the creative power of your own thoughts. Your journey to financial freedom begins now.
