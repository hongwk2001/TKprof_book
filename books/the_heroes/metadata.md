## Book Overview & Blurb

### English
Step into the mythical golden age of Ancient Greece with Charles Kingsley’s celebrated classic, *The Heroes: Greek Fairy Tales*. Specially crafted to bring the epic wonders of Greek antiquity to life, this collection presents three unforgettable legends:

1. **Perseus**: The brave youth who ventures to the ends of the earth armed with the winged sandals of Hermes and the shield of Athena to slay Medusa the Gorgon and rescue Andromeda from the sea monster.
2. **The Argonauts**: Jason and his courageous band of heroes—including Hercules, Orpheus, and Castor and Pollux—who brave treacherous clashing rocks and magical trials aboard the ship *Argo* to claim the fabled Golden Fleece.
3. **Theseus**: The legendary prince who lifts the great stone, purges the road to Athens of ruthless bandits and monsters, enters the dread Labyrinth of Crete to defeat the fearsome Minotaur, and guides his people.

Kingsley's timeless retellings weave noble ideals of courage, honor, perseverance, and humility into vivid, spellbinding adventures that have enchanted generations of readers and listeners.
