## Description
Written in 49 AD to his friend Paulinus, Seneca's *On the Shortness of Life* is the ultimate Stoic manifesto on how to reclaim your time. Seneca argues that life is long enough if you know how to use it—yet most people squander their days on trivium, ambition, and endless busyness. This modernized edition presents Seneca's urgent wisdom in high-impact English prose for contemporary readers and listeners.
