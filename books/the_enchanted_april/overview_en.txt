A Note to the Reader

Welcome to this modernized edition of The Enchanted April, Elizabeth von Arnim's beloved 1922 novel about transformation, renewal, and the magic of a Mediterranean spring.

The story begins in the dark, rainy streets of London, where two mismatched women—Mrs. Wilkins and Mrs. Arbuthnot—spot a small advertisement in the newspaper offering a medieval Italian castle to rent for the month of April. Driven by a desperate need for a rest and an escape from their dreary lives and husbands, they decide to take a leap of faith. To share the cost, they invite two other strangers to join them: the beautiful but exhausted young socialite Lady Caroline, and the stern, elderly widow Mrs. Fisher.

What follows is a beautiful, lighthearted comedy of manners. As the four women settle into the sun-drenched gardens of San Salvatore, surrounded by wistaria, lilies, and the blue sea, the beauty of their surroundings begins to work a subtle magic on each of them. Old grudges fade, heavy hearts grow light, and unexpected love begins to bloom.

In this edition, we have modernized the early 20th-century vocabulary and simplified complex sentence structures to make the story highly accessible and flowing for modern readers, students, and language learners, while preserving the full spirit, humor, and details of the original classic. We hope it brings a touch of sunshine to your day.
