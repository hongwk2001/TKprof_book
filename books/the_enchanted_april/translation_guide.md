# Translation Guide: The Enchanted April (마법에 걸린 4월)

This guide tracks the translation rules, styling guidelines, and a term dictionary to ensure a consistent, high-quality, and TTS-compliant Korean translation of **The Enchanted April** by Elizabeth von Arnim (1922).

---

## 🎭 The Web Novel Theme: Healing, Escapism, and Cozy Friendship (로맨스/힐링 판타지)

**Why it fits the Korean market:**
*The Enchanted April* mirrors the highly popular Korean "Healing & Cozy" (힐링물) web novel trend. Four women, each trapped in exhausting routines, toxic societal pressures, or cold, unloving marriages in dreary London, decide to run away together to a sunny, medieval Italian castle. The story revolves around their personal healing, blooming friendships, and finding happiness amidst the beautiful flowers and sea of San Salvatore.

**Style Goal:**
Translate into a natural, engaging, and modern Korean prose style that reads like a premium healing web novel while retaining the charming, slightly sarcastic wit of the original author.

---

## 🎧 Formatting & TTS Compatibility Rules
*   **No Parentheses for Explanations**: Avoid parentheses for explanations (e.g., do not write "위스테리아(등나무꽃)"). TTS engines read aloud punctuation ("괄호 열고... 괄호 닫고"), which ruins audiobook immersion.
*   **Natural Integration**: Weave explanations directly into the sentence (e.g., write "향기로운 보라색 등나무꽃").
*   **Pronunciation over Literal Translation**: For English terms or names that sound awkward when literally translated, use clear, readable transliterations.
