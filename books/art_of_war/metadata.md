## Description
Written in the 5th century BC, Sun Tzu's *The Art of War* remains the definitive treatise on military strategy, leadership, and conflict resolution. This modernized edition presents Sun Tzu's timeless tactical wisdom in clear, dynamic English prose optimized for modern readers and audiobook listeners alike.
