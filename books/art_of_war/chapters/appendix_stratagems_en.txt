=== Chapter 1: Winning Stratagems (승전계 / 勝戰計) ===

1. Fool the Emperor to Cross the Sea (만천과해 / 瞞天過海)
Pinyin: Mán tiān guò hǎi
Mask your true, deep intentions with everyday, normal, or routine actions. By making your movements look ordinary and unthreatening, the enemy lowers their guard, allowing you to execute your real plan in plain sight.
> Original Commentary: 备周则意怠，常见则不疑。阴在阳之内，不在阳之对。太阳，太阴。

2. Besiege Wei to Rescue Zhao (위위구조 / 圍魏救趙)
Pinyin: Wéi Wèi jiù Zhào
Avoid attacking a strong enemy directly where they are prepared. Instead, attack a vital interest, supply line, or weak point elsewhere to force them to retreat or redirect their forces to defend it.
> Original Commentary: 共敌不如分敌，敌阳不如敌阴。

3. Kill with a Borrowed Sword (차도살인 / 借刀殺人)
Pinyin: Jiè dāo shā rén
Cause an ally, a third party, or even the enemy's own internal factions to execute your plans, thereby conserving your own strength and avoiding direct blame or retaliation.
> Original Commentary: 敌已明，友未定，引友杀敌。不自出力，以《损》推演。

4. Await the Exhausted Enemy at Your Ease (이일대노 / 以逸待勞)
Pinyin: Yǐ yì dài láo
Choose the time and place of the battle to conserve your strength while forcing the enemy to travel long distances, waste energy, or exhaust themselves before you strike.
> Original Commentary: 困敌之势，不以战。损刚益柔。

5. Loot a Burning House (진화타겁 / 趁火打劫)
Pinyin: Chèn huǒ dǎ jié
Exploit an enemy's internal crisis, division, or misfortune. When they are overwhelmed by internal conflict or disaster, strike them while they are at their weakest.
> Original Commentary: 敌之害大，就势取利。刚决柔也。

6. Clamor in the East, Attack in the West (성동격서 / 聲東擊西)
Pinyin: Shēng dōng jī xī
Create a massive diversion, noise, or feint in one direction to draw the enemy's attention and defensive forces, then launch your true attack where they are unprepared.
> Original Commentary: 敌志乱萃，不虞。坤下兑上之象。利涉大川。

=== Chapter 2: Enemy Dealing Stratagems (적전계 / 敵戰計) ===

7. Create Something from Nothing (무중생유 / 無中生有)
Pinyin: Wú zhōng shēng yǒu
Use false appearances, illusions, or rumors to make the enemy believe a non-existent threat is real. Once they react to the illusion, strike them with a real attack that they dismiss as just another illusion.
> Original Commentary: 诳也，非诳也，实其所诳也。少阴、太阴、太阳。

8. Openly Repair the Walkway, but Sneak through the Passage of Chencang (암도진창 / 暗渡陳倉)
Pinyin: Àn dù Chéncāng
Direct the enemy's attention to an obvious, public, and slow-moving project (such as rebuilding a road or pathway) while executing your real, rapid maneuver through a hidden route.
> Original Commentary: 示之以动，利其静而有主，益动而巽。

9. Observe the Fire on the Opposite Shore (격안관화 / 隔岸觀火)
Pinyin: Gé àn guān huǒ
Delay entering a conflict when the enemy is experiencing internal strife. Allow their factions to fight amongst themselves and exhaust each other before you step in to claim victory.
> Original Commentary: 阳乖序乱，阴协收集。顺以动豫，豫顺以动。

10. Hide a Dagger Behind a Smile (소리장도 / 笑裏藏刀)
Pinyin: Xiào lǐ cáng dāo
Warmly charm and flatter your enemy, offering gifts or peace treaties to earn their trust and lower their suspicion, while secretly preparing a sudden, lethal blow.
> Original Commentary: 信而安之，阴以图之。备而后动，勿使有变。刚中柔外也。

11. Sacrifice the Plum Tree to Save the Peach Tree (이대도강 / 李代桃僵)
Pinyin: Lǐ dài táo jiāng
Accept minor losses, sacrifices, or temporary setbacks in one area to preserve your core strength, protect a vital asset, or achieve a long-term strategic goal.
> Original Commentary: 势必有损，损阴以益阳。

12. Take the Opportunity to Pilfer a Goat (순수견양 / 順手牽羊)
Pinyin: Shùn shǒu qiān yáng
Remain flexible and opportunistic. Even if you are executing a larger plan, seize any minor advantage, resource, or opening that presents itself along the way.
> Original Commentary: 微隙在所必乘，微利在所必得。少阴、少阳。

=== Chapter 3: Attacking Stratagems (공전계 / 攻戰計) ===

13. Beat the Grass to Startle the Snake (타초경사 / 打草驚蛇)
Pinyin: Dǎ cǎo jīng shé
Launch a small, probing attack or create a disturbance to force a hidden enemy to react. Their reaction will reveal their true position, strength, or intentions.
> Original Commentary: 疑以叩实，察而后动。复者，阴之媒也。

14. Borrow a Corpse to Resurrect the Soul (차시환혼 / 借屍還魂)
Pinyin: Jiè shī huán hún
Revive a forgotten idea, an outdated custom, a defunct organization, or a historical symbol and use it to gather support or execute your current objective.
> Original Commentary: 有用者，不可借。不能用者，求借。借不能用者而用之。匪我求童蒙，童蒙求我。

15. Lure the Tiger off its Mountain Lair (조호이산 / 調虎離山)
Pinyin: Diào hǔ lí shān
Draw the enemy out of their well-defended stronghold, familiar terrain, or position of strength into a vulnerable, open space where you have the advantage.
> Original Commentary: 待天以困之，用人以诱之。往蹇来连。

16. In Order to Capture, One Must Let Loose (욕금고종 / 欲擒故縱)
Pinyin: Yù qín gù zòng
Do not corner a defeated enemy too tightly, or they will fight to the death with desperate strength. Let them believe they have a chance to escape, causing them to lose their fighting spirit and scatter, making them easy to capture later.
> Original Commentary: 逼则反兵，走则减势。紧随勿迫。累其气力，消其斗志，散而后擒。兵不血刃。需，有孚，光。

17. Toss out a Brick to Attract Jade (포전인옥 / 拋磚引玉)
Pinyin: Pāo zhuān yǐn yù
Offer a small, cheap, or seemingly useless bait or compromise to entice the enemy into giving up something of immense value or making a major strategic mistake.
> Original Commentary: 类以诱之，击蒙也。

18. Catch the Ringleader First to Nab the Bandits (금적금왕 / 擒賊擒王)
Pinyin: Qín zéi qín wáng
Focus your effort on neutralizing the enemy's leader, command structure, or primary source of power. Once the head is removed, the rest of the organization will fall apart.
> Original Commentary: 摧其坚，夺其魁，以解其体。龙战于野，其道穷也。

=== Chapter 4: Chaos Stratagems (혼전계 / 混戰計) ===

19. Steal the Firewood from Under the Cauldron (부저추신 / 釜底抽薪)
Pinyin: Fǔ dǐ chōu xīn
Do not attack the enemy's strength directly. Instead, systematically destroy their underlying support systems, supplies, funding, or public morale to weaken them from the ground up.
> Original Commentary: 刚能柔乘，本自弱也。

20. Fish in Troubled Waters (혼수모어 / 混水摸魚)
Pinyin: Hùn shuǐ mō yú
Create confusion, chaos, or noise to disrupt the enemy's communication and organization, then take advantage of the disarray to achieve your objective unnoticed.
> Original Commentary: 乘其阴乱，顾其弱而夺之。意乘巽。

21. Slough off the Cicada's Golden Shell (금선탈각 / 金蟬脫殼)
Pinyin: Jīn chán tuō qiáo
Leave behind false appearances, decoys, or dummy forces to make the enemy believe you are still present, while you secretly withdraw or relocate your main force.
> Original Commentary: 存其形，完其势。友不疑，敌不动。巽而止蛊。

22. Close the Door to Catch the Thief (관문착적 / 關門捉賊)
Pinyin: Guān mén zhuō zéi
Completely surround and trap the enemy before launching your final attack, cutting off all avenues of escape to prevent them from regrouping or fleeing to fight again.
> Original Commentary: 小敌困之。剥，不利有攸往。

23. Befriend the Distant Enemy while Attacking the Neighbor (원교근공 / 遠交近攻)
Pinyin: Yuǎn jiāo jìn gōng
Form temporary alliances with nations or competitors who are far away to prevent them from interfering while you conquer or defeat your immediate neighbors.
> Original Commentary: 形禁势格，利从近取，害以远隔。上火下泽。

24. Borrow a Road to Conquer Guo (가도벌괵 / 假道伐虢)
Pinyin: Jiǎ dào fá Guó
Ask a smaller or weaker ally for passage or resources to attack a common enemy. Once that enemy is defeated, turn your forces on the ally who granted you passage and absorb their territory.
> Original Commentary: 两大之间，敌胁以从，我威以往。

=== Chapter 5: Gaining Ground Stratagems (병전계 / 竝戰計) ===

25. Replace the Beams with Rotten Timbers (투량환주 / 偷梁換柱)
Pinyin: Tōu liáng huàn zhù
Secretly replace the enemy's key structures, advisors, or critical components with your own weak, corrupt, or loyal substitutes, causing their entire foundation to collapse from within.
> Original Commentary: 频更其阵，抽其劲旅，待其自败，而后乘之。曳其轮也。

26. Point at the Mulberry Tree and Curse the Acacia (지상매괴 / 指桑罵槐)
Pinyin: Zhǐ sāng mà huái
Discipline, warn, or instruct your own followers or subordinates indirectly by loudly criticizing or punishing someone else. This asserts authority without causing direct offense or rebellion.
> Original Commentary: 大者示以威力，小者怀以言语。一步一趋，以《师》推演。

27. Feign Madness Without Becoming Insane (가치부전 / 假癡不癲)
Pinyin: Jiǎ chī bù diān
Pretend to be foolish, incompetent, weak, or uninterested in order to escape the enemy's suspicion and surveillance, while maintaining absolute focus and preparing your strategy in secret.
> Original Commentary: 宁伪作不知不为，不伪作聪明妄动。静不露机，云雷屯也。

28. Ascend the Roof and Remove the Ladder (상옥추제 / 上屋抽梯)
Pinyin: Shàng wū chōu tī
Lure the enemy deep into your territory or commit them to a specific line of action using false promises or easy gains, then cut off their retreat and isolate them completely.
> Original Commentary: 假之以便，唆之以利。致其深入，断其后路，以《乾》之《姤》推演。

29. Deck the Tree with False Blossoms (수상개화 / 樹上開花)
Pinyin: Shù shàng kāi huā
Use decorations, flags, decoys, or theatrics to make a small, weak force appear large, powerful, and imposing, thereby deterring an enemy attack through sheer bluffing.
> Original Commentary: 借局布势，力小势大。蓬飞依天。

30. Make the Guest Become the Host (반객위주 / 反客為主)
Pinyin: Fǎn kè wéi zhǔ
Infiltrate the enemy's camp or organization initially as a guest, assistant, or minor partner, then gradually build influence, gain control of key assets, and eventually usurp the leadership.
> Original Commentary: 乘隙插足，扼其主机，渐之进也。

=== Chapter 6: Desperate Stratagems (패전계 / 敗戰計) ===

31. The Beauty Stratagem (미인계 / 美人計)
Pinyin: Měi rén jì
Send beautiful people, luxuries, or enticing distractions to the enemy leadership. This breeds indulgence, lazyness, and internal jealousy, distracting them from their duties and weakening their resolve.
> Original Commentary: 兵强者，攻其将。将智者，伐其情。将弱兵颓，其国自垮。利用熄。

32. The Empty City Stratagem (공성계 / 空城計)
Pinyin: Kōng chéng jì
When completely defenseless, open your gates and act calm, relaxed, and welcoming. This unexpected behavior makes a suspicious enemy fear a complex ambush, prompting them to retreat.
> Original Commentary: 虚者虚之，疑中生疑。刚柔不齐，以《解》推演。

33. The Strategy of Sowing Discord (반간계 / 反間計)
Pinyin: Fǎn jiàn jì
Feed false information or rumors to the enemy's spies, or make it appear that their loyal officers are secretly plotting against them, causing the enemy to distrust and eliminate their own best people.
> Original Commentary: 疑中之疑。比自内，不自失也。

34. The Strategy of Self-Torture (고육계 / 苦肉計)
Pinyin: Kǔ ròu jì
Inflict harm or humiliation upon yourself or a loyal subordinate to convince the enemy that you have suffered a genuine fall out or defeat, making them trust your fake defection.
> Original Commentary: 人不自害，受害必真。真杰假也，以《坎》之《乾》推演。

35. The Strategy of Combining Tactics (연환계 / 連環計)
Pinyin: Lián huán jì
Link several different stratagems together in a continuous chain. If one stratagem succeeds, it sets up the next; if one is discovered, the others remain in motion to lock the enemy down.
> Original Commentary: 将多兵众，不可以敌。使其自累，以杀其势。在师中吉，承天宠也。

36. If All Else Fails, Retreat (주위상 / 走為上)
Pinyin: Zǒu wéi shàng
When facing an overwhelming force with no chance of victory, retreat immediately. Preserving your strength and army intact is the only way to ensure you can fight and win another day.
> Original Commentary: 全羽保兵，避大劲也。
