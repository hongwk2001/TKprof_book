# Introduction

The *Epic of Gilgamesh* is one of the oldest known works of literature in the world. Originally inscribed on clay tablets in ancient Mesopotamia (modern-day Iraq) over four thousand years ago, it tells the story of Gilgamesh, the mighty and arrogant king of the city of Uruk. 

Part history, part myth, this epic follows Gilgamesh's transformation from a cruel tyrant to a wise ruler. It is a deeply human story about the bonds of friendship, the fear of death, and the quest for immortality. Through his adventures with his wild companion Enkidu—battling monsters, encountering gods, and surviving a great flood—Gilgamesh ultimately discovers what it truly means to be human and to leave a lasting legacy.

This modernized edition has been carefully adapted to provide an accessible and engaging narrative for contemporary readers while preserving the profound themes and adventure of the original ancient texts.
