Chapter 14

"Eventually, I learned the history of my friends.

"The old man's name was De Lacey. He came from a wealthy and respected family in Paris. His son Felix served the country, and his daughter Agatha was a noble lady. They lived in luxury and had many friends.

"They lost everything because of Safie's father, a Turkish merchant living in Paris. The French government unfairly threw him into prison and sentenced him to death. They punished him because of his religion and wealth, not because he committed a crime. Felix attended the trial and was furious at the injustice. He swore to help the merchant escape.

"Felix visited the prison at night. The merchant promised him great wealth, but Felix refused the money. However, when Felix met Safie, they fell in love. The merchant promised that Felix could marry Safie as soon as they reached safety.

"Safie's mother had been a Christian Arab who taught Safie to value freedom and independence. Safie hated the idea of returning to Turkey, where women were locked away in harems and denied basic rights. She wanted to marry a Christian and stay in Europe.

"Felix successfully broke the merchant out of prison. They fled across the mountains to Italy. The merchant secretly hated the idea of his daughter marrying a Christian, but he played along because he needed Felix's help. Meanwhile, the French government discovered Felix's plot. They threw the old De Lacey and Agatha into a terrible, dark dungeon.

"When Felix heard the news, he rushed back to Paris to save his family, leaving Safie safely in Italy. But he failed. After five months in prison, the family lost all their wealth and were exiled from France forever.

"They found a small, miserable cottage in Germany. To make matters worse, the treacherous merchant broke his promise. As soon as he realized Felix was poor, he took Safie and fled to Turkey, leaving Felix heartbroken and penniless.

"That was why Felix was so sad when I first saw him. But Safie refused to obey her father. She took some money and jewels and traveled alone to Germany. After a long, dangerous journey where her only companion died of illness, she finally found Felix's cottage. Her arrival brought joy back to his life."
