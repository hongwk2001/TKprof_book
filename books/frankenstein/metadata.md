## Description
Mary Shelley's seminal 1818 novel *Frankenstein* explores the consequences of scientific hubris and the quest for creation. When Victor Frankenstein breathes life into a creature constructed from scavenged parts, he is instantly repulsed by his creation, setting off a tragic cycle of rejection, vengeance, and existential dread.
