Introduction to Frankenstein

Welcome to Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley's seminal masterpiece of gothic horror and science fiction: "Frankenstein. Or, the Modern Prometheus".

First published anonymously in 1818, Shelley's haunting story of ambition, creation, and isolation arose from a famous ghost story competition in Geneva. It has since become one of the most enduring myths of the modern age. Through the tragic conflict between the young scientist Victor Frankenstein and the nameless creature he brings to life, the novel explores profound questions of scientific responsibility, social rejection, and what it truly means to be human.

In this modernized edition, Shelley's romantic and atmospheric prose has been carefully refined into clear, accessible language optimized for modern readers and English language learners alike, while retaining all the psychological depth and emotional intensity of the original work.
