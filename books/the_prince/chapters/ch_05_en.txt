Chapter 5
How to Govern Cities or Principalities That Lived Under Their Own Laws Before Being Conquered

When you conquer a state that is used to living freely under its own laws, you have three options to keep it. The first option is to completely destroy it. The second is to go and live there in person. The third is to let the people keep their own laws while forcing them to pay a tax. If you choose this third option, you must set up a small government run by a few loyal locals. This new government will know that it only exists because of you. It will do everything in its power to support your rule, since it relies entirely on your friendship and influence to survive. Ultimately, if you want to control a city that is used to freedom, the easiest way to hold it is through its own citizens.

Consider the historical examples of the Spartans and the Romans. The Spartans controlled Athens and Thebes by setting up small local governments, but they eventually lost both cities. The Romans took a different approach. To keep control of Capua, Carthage, and Numantia, they simply destroyed them, and they never lost them. Later, the Romans tried to govern Greece the same way the Spartans did, by letting it be free and keeping its own laws. This failed. To finally secure Greece, the Romans were forced to destroy many of its cities. The brutal truth is that there is no safe way to hold a free city other than by destroying it. If you conquer a city that is used to freedom and you do not ruin it, you should expect it to ruin you. Whenever it rebels, the people will always rally around the ideas of liberty and their ancient rights. Neither the passage of time nor any favors you grant will ever make them forget their freedom. No matter what you do or what precautions you take, the people will always remember that name of liberty. Unless you scatter the citizens and tear them apart, they will seize any opportunity to revolt. Just look at Pisa. They rebelled after being held in slavery by the Florentines for a hundred years.

Things are completely different when a city or country is already used to living under a prince. If you conquer them and wipe out the old ruling family, the people are left without a leader. They are already trained to obey, but they can never agree on choosing a new leader from among themselves. They simply do not know how to live in freedom. Because of this, they are very slow to take up arms against you. A new prince can easily win them over and secure their loyalty. Republics, on the other hand, are full of life, hatred, and a burning desire for revenge. The memory of their former liberty will never let them rest. Therefore, the only truly safe ways to handle a former republic are to wipe it out completely or to go live there yourself.
