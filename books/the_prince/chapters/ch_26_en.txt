Chapter 26
An Exhortation To Liberate Italy From The Barbarians

I have carefully considered everything discussed so far. I have asked myself whether the present times are right for a new prince. I have wondered if there are elements that give a wise and capable leader the opportunity to introduce a new order. Such a new order would bring him honor and do good for the people of this country. It seems to me that so many things are coming together to favor a new prince right now. In fact, I have never known a time more fit than the present.

As I said before, it was necessary for the Israelites to be enslaved in Egypt to reveal the ability of Moses. It was necessary for the Persians to be oppressed by the Medes to discover the greatness of Cyrus. It was necessary for the Athenians to be scattered to show the capabilities of Theseus. In the same way, right now, it was necessary for Italy to be reduced to her current extreme misery to discover the Virtù of an Italian spirit. She had to be more enslaved than the Hebrews, more oppressed than the Persians, and more scattered than the Athenians. She had to be without a leader, without order, beaten, stripped down, torn apart, overrun, and forced to endure every kind of desolation.

Recently, there was a spark of hope in one man. This made us think he was ordained by God for our redemption. However, at the peak of his career, Fortuna rejected him. Now Italy, left almost completely lifeless, waits for someone to heal her wounds. She waits for a leader to end the ravaging and plundering of Lombardy. She waits for someone to stop the swindling and heavy taxing in the Kingdom of Naples and in Tuscany. She needs a leader to cleanse the sores that have festered for so long. You can see how she begs God to send someone who will deliver her from these cruel wrongs and barbarous insults. You can also see that she is completely ready and willing to follow a banner, if only someone will raise it.

Right now, there is no one in whom she can place more hope than in your illustrious house. Your family has the courage and Fortuna, favored by God and by the Church, of which it is now the head. Your house can easily become the leader of this redemption. This will not be difficult if you keep in mind the actions and lives of the men I just named. Those men were great and wonderful, but they were still only men. None of them had a better opportunity than the one offered today. Their enterprises were no more just or easier than this one. God was no more their friend than He is yours.

We have great justice on our side, because a war is just when it is necessary. Arms are sacred when they are our only hope. There is immense willingness here, and where the willingness is great, the difficulties cannot be great, as long as you follow the example of the men I have highlighted. Beyond this, God has shown extraordinary signs without equal. The sea has divided. A cloud has led the way. The rock has poured out water. It has rained manna. Everything has contributed to your greatness. You must do the rest. God is not willing to do everything, because He does not want to take away our free will and that share of glory which rightfully belongs to us.

It is no surprise if none of the Italians I mentioned before were able to accomplish what is now expected of your illustrious house. Over so many revolutions and military campaigns in Italy, it has always seemed as though our military Virtù was completely exhausted. This happened because the old systems were broken, and no one knew how to invent new ones. Nothing brings a man more honor than establishing new laws and new institutions when he first rises to power. When these foundations are solid and dignified, they make him revered and admired. In Italy, there is no shortage of opportunities to introduce these new forms.

There is tremendous strength in the limbs here, even if the head is weak. Look closely at the duels and hand-to-hand combat. You will see how superior the Italians are in strength, dexterity, and cleverness. But when it comes to whole armies, they do not compare. This springs entirely from the weakness of the leaders. Those who are capable refuse to obey. Everyone thinks they know best. Until now, no one has stood out enough, either through their own Virtù or Fortuna, to make the others yield. This is why, for the past twenty years of constant fighting, an all-Italian army has always failed miserably. The first proof of this was at Il Taro, followed by Alessandria, Capua, Genoa, Vaila, Bologna, and Mestri.

Therefore, if your illustrious house wishes to follow those remarkable men who redeemed their countries, you must first do one essential thing. As the true foundation of every enterprise, you must provide yourself with your own national forces. There can be no truer, more faithful, or better soldiers. Individually they are good, but together they will become much better when they find themselves commanded, honored, and maintained by their own prince. Therefore, it is absolutely necessary to prepare these forces so you can defend yourself against foreigners using Italian strength.

Swiss and Spanish infantry may be considered terrifying, but they both have a critical flaw. A third type of force could not only oppose them but confidently overthrow them. The Spaniards cannot stand against cavalry, and the Swiss are terrified of infantry when they meet them in close combat. Because of this, as we have seen and will see again, the Spaniards cannot resist French cavalry, and the Swiss are crushed by Spanish infantry. We may not have seen a complete proof of the latter, but there was strong evidence of it at the battle of Ravenna. There, the Spanish infantry clashed with German battalions, who use the same tactics as the Swiss. The Spaniards, using their physical agility and their small shields, got in under the long pikes of the Germans. They were safe to attack while the Germans stood helpless. If the cavalry had not charged in, the Spaniards would have slaughtered them all. Therefore, knowing the defects of both the Swiss and Spanish infantries, you can invent a new one. This new force will resist cavalry and not be afraid of infantry. You do not need to create entirely new weapons; you just need to alter the old tactics. These are exactly the kinds of innovations that give reputation and power to a new prince.

This opportunity must not be allowed to pass. Italy must finally see her liberator appear. I cannot express the overwhelming love that would greet him in all these provinces that have suffered so much from these foreign invasions. Imagine the thirst for revenge, the stubborn faith, the devotion, and the tears! What doors would be closed to him? Who would refuse to obey him? What envy would dare stand in his way? What Italian would refuse to bow to him? This barbarian rule stinks in the nostrils of every one of us. Therefore, let your illustrious house take up this charge with the courage and hope that accompany all just enterprises. Under your banner, our native country may be ennobled. Under your leadership, the words of Petrarch will finally come true:

Virtù will take up arms against fury,
and the battle will be short.
For the ancient valor is not yet dead
in Italian hearts.
