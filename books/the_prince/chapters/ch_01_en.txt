Chapter 1
How Many Kinds Of Principalities There Are, And By What Means They Are Acquired

Every state and every power that has ever ruled over people is either a republic or a principality.

Principalities are either hereditary, meaning a single family has ruled them for a long time, or they are completely new.

These new principalities fall into two categories. They are either entirely new, just like Milan was for Francesco Sforza. Or they are territories added onto the hereditary state of the prince who conquered them, just like the Kingdom of Naples was for the King of Spain.

When you acquire these new territories, they are used to one of two things. They are either accustomed to living under a prince, or they are used to living in freedom. A ruler can acquire these lands using his own army, or he can rely on the military forces of others. Ultimately, he gains control of them either through Fortuna, meaning luck and fortune, or through Virtù, meaning his own sheer ability and skill.
