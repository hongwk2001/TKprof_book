"No," she said in a calmly settled voice. "There's no need to make a fuss. You can just listen, Professor, and deliver exactly the same to them. I must go along with you and your party on the path you are leaving."

Dr. Van Helsing jumped up in surprise as if he had been shot in the head. Mumbling like a mute who ate honey, he barely managed to ask.

"No, why on earth?"

"You must take me with you unconditionally. It will be much safer for me to stay by your side, and it will also help keep all of you alive."

"I completely cannot understand, dear Madam Mina. Don't you clearly know that keeping you safe is the mission we must protect even at the risk of our lives? We are basically jumping into hellfire right now, and because of... the terrible things of the past, you are more prone to becoming his prey than any of us." He couldn't bear to continue and shut his mouth.

Instead of answering, Mina raised her finger and pointed to her scar.

"I know too. Because I know everything, I am insisting on going. Right now, as the morning sun shines brightly, I can speak to you with a sane mind, but when night comes, I might not be able to keep my sanity. I keenly know that if the Count calls me in the dark, I have to go even if I have to crawl like a dog. If he whispers to me to sneak out secretly, I will crawl to him doing whatever despicable act it takes—even completely deceiving my husband Jonathan." God surely looked down upon Mina's desperate gaze as she looked at me while spitting out those words. And if there is an angel who records human sins and punishments one by one, that noble gaze she just showed will remain shining as evidence proving her eternal honor. I couldn't even bear to open my mouth and had no choice but to squeeze her cold hand as if to crush it. It felt like my heart was being torn to shreds, so not even tears came out. She continued firmly.

"You men are brave as lions and strong as rocks. Moreover, there are many of you, so you can easily stand against the merciless power that gnaws away the patience of a weak human who has to stand guard trembling alone. Furthermore, I might be of help. The Professor can hypnotize me to figure out the Count's movements, which even I myself am completely unaware of." Dr. Van Helsing opened his mouth with a face as solemn as a funeral parlor.

"Madam Mina, you are chillingly wise as always. You, too, will go to the jaws of death with us. And we will all together grasp the goal we intend to achieve with both hands." As soon as his pledge fell, Mina tightly shut her mouth as if they had made a promise. Feeling uneasy, I stared intently at her. She had already fallen into a deep sleep with her head buried in her pillow. I walked to the window and threw up the blinds, pouring dazzling morning sunlight full into the room, but she didn't even twitch an eyelid. Van Helsing gestured quietly for us to leave the room. We crossed over to his room, and in less than a minute, Lord Godalming, Dr. Seward, and Morris joined us, rubbing their eyes. He conveyed Mina's shocking decision to the others and hastily revised the plan.

"Tomorrow morning we will leave for Varna right away. Now we have to run carrying a time bomb-like variable named Madam Mina. Oh, but Madam's soul is crystal clear and true. It must have been painfully agonizing, as if cutting her own flesh, to expose her shame and confess to us, but Madam's judgment was right a hundred, a thousand times, and thanks to it, we prevented the worst situation. We must not allow even a single second of loophole. The moment we arrive in Varna and his ship drops anchor, we must be ready to snatch his leash right away."

"How exactly do you plan to cook him?" Morris bluntly pierced the core. The Professor breathed heavily before answering.

"First, as soon as the ship arrives, we will jump straight onto the deck. Once we find the Count's coffin, I plan to place wild rose branches on top of it like a cross. And tie it tightly to seal it up. As long as the rose branches are placed there, the vampire can never crawl out. At least that's what the legends say. At first, we too must borrow the power of superstition and legends. The faith of the people from the distant past was also superstition, and superstition still has its roots in faith. Then, when a perfect opportunity comes when no one is watching, we pry open his coffin and—drive a stake through his heart, and all nightmares will be over."

"If it were me, I wouldn't wait for any opportunity and just put an end to it right away." Morris bared his teeth and spoke viciously. "As soon as I find the coffin, I'll split it open with an axe and smash that damn monster bastard. I wouldn't blink an eye even if thousands of onlookers were gathered around. Even if my head flies off the next moment because of that crazy act, I will willingly accept it!" I instinctively grabbed his tough hand, and his hand was as solid and hot as an iron fist. I hope he read the meaning of my desperate look. I really hope so.

"Excellent." Dr. Van Helsing smiled. "You are truly a man with thick guts. Quincey is a true real man. May God's blessing pour down on your boiling blood. Brothers, keep my words in mind. None of us will have our legs give out from fear or turn tail and run. I merely stated the best choice we can make—the necessity we must achieve. But honestly speaking, even I have no idea what will happen tomorrow. There are so many variables and traps that we can't guarantee anything until we actually open the lid. We will arm ourselves thoroughly from teeth to claws, and when the moment comes to cut off his breath, we will pour out all the power we have. Now, let us all tidy up our personal affairs throughout today. Make sure to clearly prepare what to leave behind for the precious people who live solely trusting us. Because none of us knows who, when, or how we will face a terrible death. I have already written my will early on and have no other luggage to pack, so I will take the lead and finish up preparing the train tickets and travel arrangements."

There was no need to chatter any longer, and we scattered to our respective rooms with grim faces. Now I will cleanly erase all my traces left on earth and prepare to willingly throw myself into the bloody fate to come...

Later.—All preparations are finished. I have also stamped my seal on my will and completed all legal procedures. If Mina survives, all my property will entirely become hers. If the two of us lose our lives side by side, our other friends who have shown us unmerited favor so far will share the remaining property.

The sun is setting soon. Mina's anxious eyes glancing out the window are squeezing my heart. The very moment the sun goes down, something hidden in her head will surely pop out. The moments of sunrise and sunset have become a blood-drying, hellish time for all of us. Because every morning and evening, a new and terrible danger raises its head. But even this terrifying pain might be a great test to lead us to victory within God's great providence. I meticulously write down all these terrible truths in every corner of my journal. My beloved wife must not know these frightening stories right now, but later, when this nightmare ends and she opens this notebook again, all the records must remain perfectly intact.

She is calling me.
