October 3-4, around midnight.— Yesterday felt like it would never end. I desperately wanted to sleep, clinging to the vague belief that when I woke up, everything would have changed, and that the change would surely be for the better. Before we parted ways, we discussed our next plan, but we came to no conclusion. The only thing we know for certain is that there is only one box of earth left, and only the Count knows where it is. If he decides to stay hidden, he could evade us for years. And during that time...! Just the thought of it is so terrifying that I barely dare to even think about it right now. But one thing is certain: if there is a perfect woman in this world, it is my poor wife who is suffering so unjustly. The affectionate pity she showed last night, the pure pity that made even my hatred for the monster feel despicable, made me love her a thousand times more. Surely, God will not allow the world to lose such a beautiful soul. This is my only hope. We are currently drifting toward a reef, and faith is our only anchor. Thank God! Mina is sleeping soundly without any nightmares. I am terrified to even imagine what kind of horrible nightmares the terrible memories she has endured might create. She hasn't looked this peaceful since the sun went down. For a while, a calm like a warm spring after a raging March storm lingered on her face. At first, I thought she looked that way because the red glow of the sunset gently enveloped her face, but for some reason, I feel there is a deeper meaning to that expression now. I am exhausted to the bone, but sleep won't come. Still, I must try to get some sleep. I have to prepare for tomorrow, and there will be no true rest for me until this matter is resolved...

A little later.— I must have dozed off for a moment. Mina sat straight up in bed and woke me with a startled expression. I hadn't made the room completely dark, so I could clearly see her figure. She pressed her hand firmly over my mouth as a warning and whispered in my ear.

"Hush! There's someone in the hallway!" I got up silently, crossed the room, and carefully opened the door.

Mr. Morris was lying wide awake on a mattress placed in the hallway right outside the door. He immediately raised his hand to signal me to be quiet and whispered.

"Hush! Go back to bed and sleep. It's okay. One of us will be here all night. We don't intend to risk any danger!"

Seeing his resolute expression and gesture, it was clear he didn't want to talk any further, so I returned and told Mina about this. Mina let out a sigh of relief, and a faint but clear smile spread across her pale face. She pulled me into a hug and whispered softly.

"Oh, you good and brave men. Thank God!" She let out a sigh and fell back asleep. I should try to sleep again, but I'm completely awake now, so I'm writing in my diary.

October 4, Morning.— Mina woke me up again during the night. This time, it was after we both had quite a good sleep. The pale light of the approaching dawn was turning the window into a distinct rectangle, and the gas flame looked like a faint speck rather than a bright disk. She spoke to me urgently.

"Go and call the professor. I need to see him right now."

"Why?" I asked.

"I've realized something. Perhaps it took shape in my mind during the night without me knowing. The professor must hypnotize me before dawn. Then I'll be able to speak freely. Hurry, dear. We don't have much time." I went to the door. Dr. Seward, who was resting on the mattress in the hallway, saw me and sprang to his feet.

"Is something wrong?" he asked in surprise.

"No," I replied. "But Mina wants to see Professor Van Helsing right away."

"I will go fetch him," he said, and hurried toward the professor's room.

Two or three minutes later, Professor Van Helsing entered the room in his dressing gown, while Mr. Morris and Lord Godalming stood at the door, asking Dr. Seward what was going on. When the professor saw Mina smiling, his worried face brightened completely, and he rubbed his hands together with joy.

"Oh, my dear Mrs. Mina, what a remarkable change. Look! Friend Jonathan, the Mrs. Mina of old has finally returned to us today!" He turned back to Mina and asked affectionately, "Now, how can I help you? You wouldn't have called me at this early hour for no reason."

"Hypnotize me!" Mina said. "Do it right now before dawn. By then, I think I will be able to speak freely without being suppressed. Hurry, there's no time!" The professor silently motioned for Mina to sit up straight in bed.

The professor stared intently into her eyes and began making slow hand gestures, alternating his hands from the top of her head downwards. Mina gazed at him without looking away for a few minutes, and during that time, my heart pounded like a hammer. I instinctively felt that some crucial moment was approaching. Slowly, her eyes closed, and Mina sat perfectly still as if she had turned to stone. Only her quietly rising and falling chest indicated that she was alive. The professor made a few more gestures and then stopped, and I saw thick beads of sweat on his forehead. Mina opened her eyes, but she seemed like a completely different person from her usual self. Her unfocused, blank eyes stared into the empty air, and her voice held a dreamy and sorrowful echo that I had never heard before. The professor raised his hand to signal us to be quiet, and then motioned for me to let the others into the room. They tiptoed in carefully, closed the door, stood at the foot of the bed, and watched silently. Mina didn't seem to notice they had entered at all. Professor Van Helsing broke the silence in a low, calm voice. It was an uninflected, tranquil voice, intended not to disrupt the flow of her thoughts.

"Where are you now?" A dry, emotionless answer returned.

"I don't know. Sleep has no fixed place." A heavy silence hung for a few minutes. Mina remained completely stiff, and the professor stared intently at her. The rest of us were careful not to even make a sound breathing. It was getting brighter outside. Dr. Van Helsing motioned for me to raise the blind without taking his eyes off Mina's face.
