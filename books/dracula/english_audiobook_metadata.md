## 5. Content rules (these caused the structural rejections before)

*   **Opening credits** contain only: *"Dracula. Written by Bram Stoker. Narrated by
    {NARRATOR}."* — no copyright, no production credits.
*   **Closing credits** contain only: *"This has been Dracula, by Bram Stoker. The end."*
    — no URLs, no review requests, no edition notes.
*   **Retail sample** is 3 minutes of real narration from chapter 1.

## 6. Known residual risk

Kokoro synthesizes at **24 kHz**, so the audio carries a ~12 kHz bandwidth ceiling.
Every free engine available locally (Kokoro, Qwen3-TTS, edge-tts) has this limit.

The previous rejection's "muffled audio quality or static" was driven mainly by the
Korean track, which was sourced from a **48 kbps** MP3 — that source is gone entirely
in this edition. If Authors Republic still cites muffled audio, the only remaining
lever is a paid 44.1/48 kHz engine (Azure Speech ≈ $20–30 for this word count).
