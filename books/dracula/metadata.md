## Description
Bram Stoker's legendary 1897 novel *Dracula* introduced the world to the quintessential vampire. Told through a series of gripping letters, diary entries, and newspaper clippings, the novel chronicles Count Dracula's relocation from Transylvania to England to spread the undead curse, and the small band of men and women led by Professor Abraham Van Helsing who pledge to destroy him. This classic is modernized for contemporary readers and ESL learners.
