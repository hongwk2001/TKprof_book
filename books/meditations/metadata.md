## 👤 About the Author

**Marcus Aurelius** (121–180 AD) was the Emperor of the Roman Empire and is remembered as one of history's most significant Stoic philosophers. Often referred to as the last of the "Five Good Emperors," his reign was marked by severe challenges, including border wars and widespread plague. His private journal—now known globally as *Meditations*—was written as a series of personal notes to guide his own conduct, reflecting a lifelong commitment to reason, duty, and self-mastery.
