Chapter Thirty: Hrothgar's Sermon on Pride

> *The Danes leave without hope, but the Geat warriors stand their ground, firmly believing in their hero's return.*

The old king Hrothgar rose. The feast-hall instantly plunged into absolute silence. 

<hrothgar>"Any leader who rules with an iron fist of truth and justice, guarding his realm and honoring the blood of the past, can look at you and see a true hero,"</hrothgar> Hrothgar bellowed. <hrothgar>"Your fame burns across the world, Beowulf! You wield immense power, yet your mind is just as sharp. Our pact stands strong. You will be a fortress for your people."</hrothgar>

He paused, his voice turning grim. <hrothgar>"King Heremod was not like you. He didn't bring victory to the Danes—he brought slaughter. Driven by blind rage, he butchered his own brothers-in-arms. The great prince isolated himself in a fortress of his own madness."</hrothgar>

Hrothgar's eyes locked onto Beowulf. <hrothgar>"God forged him stronger than any mortal, yet Heremod let his heart turn to poison. He hoarded his gold, denied his warriors their rightful spoils, and died a miserable, cursed wreck. Learn from his ruin, Beowulf! Let this old warrior's scars be your guide."</hrothgar>

The king swept his gaze across the hall. <hrothgar>"It is a terrifying wonder how God hands out power, land, and wisdom. He commands everything. He lets a king feast on victory, gifting him impenetrable fortresses and endless borders. Drunk on invincibility, the king forgets that death comes for us all."</hrothgar>

<hrothgar>"He lives untouchable—no sickness, no aging, no war. The world bows to him. But then, pride takes root like a parasite. His conscience falls into a dead sleep. And when the watchman sleeps, the Enemy strikes! A poisoned arrow pierces the king’s black heart."</hrothgar>

Hrothgar leaned forward. <hrothgar>"Suddenly, his vast empire isn't enough. Greed claws at his soul. He locks away his gold, starving his loyal warriors of their rings, forgetting that his glory was a gift from above."</hrothgar>

<hrothgar>"But the end always comes,"</hrothgar> Hrothgar warned. <hrothgar>"The king rots and dies. A new ruler steps over his corpse and throws open the treasure vaults, rewarding the bold without an ounce of fear or greed."</hrothgar>

<hrothgar>"Crush that pride, Beowulf! You are the greatest of men—choose the eternal path. Do not let arrogance master you!"</hrothgar> The old king’s voice echoed like thunder. <hrothgar>"Right now, your strength is absolute. But mark my words: sickness, or a savage blade, will strip it away. Fire, flood, a flying spear, or the crippling grip of old age will tear you down. The fire in your eyes will burn out. In the end, death conquers everyone. Even you."</hrothgar>
