Chapter Six: Face-to-Face with King Hrothgar

> *An oath to cast aside weapons, declaring a bare-handed bloodbath with the monster.*

Hrothgar, the scarred king of the Danes, took Wulfgar's report and cracked a grim smile. <hrothgar>"I knew Beowulf when he was just a whelp! His bloodline is pure steel—son of Ecgtheow, born of Geatish royalty. Now, that boy has grown into a monster of a man, and he's come hunting for a fight alongside a true ally."</hrothgar>

The king leaned forward, his eyes gleaming in the firelight. <hrothgar>"My sailors claim this warrior packs the strength of thirty men in a single fist. I swear, the gods themselves must have hurled this champion at us to crush Grendel's skull! I'll shower him in gold if he has the guts to do it."</hrothgar>

Hrothgar slammed his fist on the throne. <hrothgar>"Move! Bring the Geats in. Let them stand among our fiercest lords and know the Danes embrace them!"</hrothgar>

Wulfgar bolted to the hall's massive doors. He faced the waiting Geats and roared, <rest>"My king knows your bloodline! You've crossed the crushing sea, and he welcomes you to Heorot."</rest>

Wulfgar's gaze hardened. <rest>"You march in wearing your armor. Keep your helmets on. But leave your shields and killing steel out here until the king has taken your measure."</rest>

Beowulf rose like a titan of war, his elite squad falling into line behind him. With a sharp gesture, he ordered a handful of his men to hold the perimeter and guard their weapons.

The rest surged forward. Wulfgar led the heavy-armored strike team straight into the belly of Heorot. Beowulf didn't flinch. He marched like a storm tearing through the great hall, his helmet gleaming with raw menace, until he planted his boots dead center before the Danish king.
