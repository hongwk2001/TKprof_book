A Note to the Reader

Welcome to this modernized edition of The Secret Garden, Frances Hodgson Burnett's beloved 1911 classic about healing, friendship, and the transformative power of nature.

The story centers on Mary Lennox, a sickly, neglected, and bad-tempered young girl born in India. When a cholera outbreak leaves her orphaned, she is sent to live with her reclusive uncle at the gloomy Misselthwaite Manor in Yorkshire, England. Left mostly to herself in the vast estate, Mary's curiosity is sparked by stories of a mysterious garden that has been locked shut for ten years. 

With the help of a friendly robin, Mary finds the key and slips inside the secret garden. Together with Dickon, a kind local boy who has a magical way with wild animals, and Colin, her uncle's bedridden, anxious son who believes he is destined to die, Mary works to bring the garden back to life. As the roses and flowers bloom in the spring sunshine, the children experience their own miraculous transformations, discovering health, joy, and the magic of hope.

In this edition, we have modernized the early 20th-century vocabulary and simplified complex sentence structures to make the story highly accessible and flowing for modern readers, students, and language learners. We have also gently softened the heavy Yorkshire dialect of characters like Dickon, Martha, and Ben Weatherstaff to ensure readability while preserving all of their rustic charm, warmth, and character. We hope this timeless tale of renewal brings joy and inspiration to your day.
