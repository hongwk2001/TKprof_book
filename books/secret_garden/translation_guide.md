# Translation Guide: The Secret Garden (비밀의 화원)

This guide tracks the translation rules, styling guidelines, and a term dictionary to ensure a consistent, high-quality, and TTS-compliant Korean translation of **The Secret Garden** by Frances Hodgson Burnett (1911).

---

## 🎭 Style & Tone: Healing, Nature, and Emotional Growth (성장/힐링 판타지)

**Korean Market Fit:**
*The Secret Garden* is a masterpiece that perfectly aligns with the popular Korean "Healing & Growth" (힐링/성장물) literary genre. The narrative focuses on the transformation of two neglected, angry, and sickly children (Mary and Colin) as they find a locked garden and, by caring for it alongside the nature-loving country boy Dickon, restore their health, open their hearts, and discover the beauty of friendship.

**Style Goal:**
- Translate into a warm, evocative, and classic Korean prose style that reads elegantly like a premium literary edition while retaining Burnett's rich, poetic, and nature-centric descriptions.
- Use a natural mix of **십시오체** (for polite, formal dialogue), **해요체** (for softer, familiar dialogue), and **해라체** (for narrator and children's speech when appropriate).

---

## 🎧 Formatting & TTS Compatibility Rules
*   **No Parentheses for Explanations**: Avoid parentheses for explanations (e.g., do not write "로빈(붉은가슴울새)" or "아야(유모)"). TTS engines read aloud punctuation ("괄호 열고... 괄호 닫고"), which ruins audiobook immersion.
*   **Natural Integration**: Weave explanations directly into the sentence (e.g., write "귀여운 붉은가슴울새" or "인도인 유모 아야").
*   **Pronunciation over Literal Translation**: For English terms or names, use clear, standard Korean transliterations.
