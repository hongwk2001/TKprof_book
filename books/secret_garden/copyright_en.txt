About This Modernized Edition

This edition of Frances Hodgson Burnett's 1911 classic has been carefully adapted and modernized by TKPROF LLC. 

Our modernization process focuses on:
- Simplifying Victorian and early 20th-century language to flow naturally for modern readers and ESL/EFL learners.
- Breaking down long, complex sentence structures into clear, active, and engaging English.
- Softening heavy rustic dialects just enough to ensure readability for language learners while preserving character charm.
- Preserving 100% of the original characters, plot, setting, and emotional depth of the story.
