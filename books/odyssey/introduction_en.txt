About This Edition

Welcome to this modernized edition of Homer's *Odyssey*, ancient Greece's greatest adventure and a foundational text of Western literature.

After the Trojan War, the wily hero Odysseus sets sail for his island home of Ithaca, where his beloved wife Penelope and son Telemachus await. But his journey is anything but smooth. Enraged when Odysseus blinds his son, the Cyclops, the sea god Poseidon curses him with ten years of relentless trials and storms, determined to prevent his return. From the one-eyed giant Polyphemus and the Sirens whose enchanting songs lure sailors to their doom, to the sorceress Circe who turns men into swine, and the monstrous Scylla and Charybdis, Odysseus faces a parade of mythical beasts and mortal dangers.

Meanwhile, back in Ithaca, chaos reigns in Odysseus's royal palace. Dozens of arrogant suitors, convinced Odysseus is dead, feast daily, squandering his wealth as they vie for his crown and Queen Penelope's hand. His son, Telemachus, now a young man, embarks on a perilous quest to find his father, while Penelope, ever resourceful, cleverly fends off the suitors' relentless pressure, clinging to hope for her husband's return. All the while, Athena, goddess of wisdom and war, lends her unseen aid, subtly guiding the House of Odysseus through their darkest hours.

This new edition reimagines a challenging classic epic in a modern novel style, making it more accessible and thrilling for contemporary readers, including young adults. We've paid meticulous attention to crafting natural, conversational language that flows smoothly and comfortably when read aloud, perfect for audiobooks and text-to-speech. Yet, we've carefully preserved the profound depth and vivid adventurous spirit of Homer's original work. Join Odysseus on an unforgettable journey into the myths of the vast Mediterranean.
