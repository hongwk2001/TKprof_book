## Description
This unified volume presents Lucius Annaeus Seneca's two most famous Stoic dialogues: *On the Shortness of Life* (De Brevitate Vitae) and *On Happiness* (De Vita Beata). In *On the Shortness of Life*, Seneca delivers a powerful, urgent warning against squandering our most precious resource—time—on trivial distractions, urging us to master the present. In *On Happiness*, he explores the true nature of joy and virtue, arguing that true happiness is found by living in alignment with reason and nature, while also detailing a sophisticated philosophical defense of the proper use of material wealth. Styled and modernized for contemporary readers, this edition is optimized for both clear reading and effortless audiobook (TTS) listening.
