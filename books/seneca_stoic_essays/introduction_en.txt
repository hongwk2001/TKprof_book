Introduction to Seneca's Stoic Essays

Welcome to Lucius Annaeus Seneca's classic Stoic works: "On the Shortness of Life" (De Brevitate Vitae) and "On Happiness" (De Vita Beata).

This unified volume combines two of Seneca's most powerful and enduring treatises on how to live. Together, they form a comprehensive guide to Stoic philosophy, focusing on the preservation of our most precious resource—time—and the pursuit of true, enduring happiness through reason and virtue.

In "On the Shortness of Life," Seneca forcefully argues that life is not short, but that we waste much of it on trivial pursuits. He teaches us how to reclaim our time and live fully in the present.

In "On Happiness," Seneca explores the relationship between virtue and pleasure, arguing that true joy comes from living in accordance with nature. He also addresses his critics, explaining the role of wealth in a philosophical life.

This premium modernized edition presents these classic dialogues styled and structured for contemporary readers and listeners seeking focus, clarity, and purpose.
