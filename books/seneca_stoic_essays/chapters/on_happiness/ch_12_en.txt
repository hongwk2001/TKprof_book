[Chapter 12]
He answers that they are not at peace because many things distract their thoughts and their minds become anxious due to conflicting opinions. I admit this is true. But even these foolish and changeable people get huge amounts of Pleasure. They will definitely regret it soon. We must admit that they feel no pain while they do this. They also fail to make right judgments. We must admit that they are caught in pleasant madness and laugh while talking nonsense. On the other hand, the Pleasure of wise people is gentle and dignified. It is sometimes so moderate that it is almost dry and barely visible. People do not invite that Pleasure to come. It does not receive a huge welcome when it comes on its own. The people who receive Pleasure do not greet it with any joy. They just mix Pleasure into their lives like a funny short play between serious duties. Pleasure fills the empty spaces of their lives. So do not let them try to tie together things that do not match anymore. Do not let them try to connect Pleasure with Virtue. This is just a mistake of pleasing the worst people. The reckless drunkard who is always drunk and smells like wine believes he lives with Virtue. He believes this because he knows he lives with Pleasure. He hears that Pleasure cannot exist apart from Virtue. As a result, he gives his vice the name of wisdom and proudly shows off everything he should hide. People do not become wild because of the teachings of Epicurus. Instead, bad people hide their wildness inside the arms of philosophy. They flock to the school where they can hear praise for Pleasure. They do not consider how serious and moderate the Pleasure Epicurus spoke about really is. They just run to the name of Epicurus and try to get a cloak to protect and hide their vices. As a result, they lose the only virtue their evil lives had. That virtue was the shame of doing wrong. Now they praise the things they used to be ashamed of and boast about their vices. Shame can never recover itself when shameful laziness receives an honorable name. The praise your school pours on Pleasure is harmful because people do not notice the honorable parts of the teaching. The corrupt parts catch the eyes of everyone.
