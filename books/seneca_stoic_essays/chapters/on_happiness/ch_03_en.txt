[Chapter 3]
Let us find a blessing that is not just nice on the outside. Let us find one that is completely sound and excellent on the inside. Even its most hidden parts should be the most beautiful. Let us dig it up. It is not far from us. We can find it. We only need to know where to reach our hands. But in reality, we act as if we are in the dark. We reach far past the things closest to us. We bump into the exact thing we want without realizing it. But I will skip the opinions of other philosophers so I do not lead you astray. Listing and rejecting all of them would take too much time. Listen to the opinion of our Stoic school instead. But when I say our opinion, I do not tie myself to just one leader of the Stoic school. I also have the right to form my own opinion. Therefore, I will follow the authority of some of them. But I will ask others to explain their meaning. Perhaps someone will ask my opinion after I report all the opinions of previous people. I will not contradict any decisions of my elders. I will say that I will also add something to their opinions. Meanwhile, I follow nature. All Stoic philosophers agree on this. True wisdom means not straying from nature. It means molding our actions according to her laws and examples. Therefore, a happy life is a life that matches its own nature. First, the mind must be healthy and constantly maintain that state. Next, it must be brave and energetic. It must endure everything with the greatest courage suited for the times it lives in. Also, we must pay attention to the body and its parts, but we must not worry too much. Furthermore, we must place proper value on all the things that decorate our life. But we must not overvalue any of them. We must enjoy the abundance that fortune provides without becoming her slaves. You will understand even if I do not say it. Unbreakable mental balance and freedom follow when we drive away everything that excites or frightens us. Instead of sensual Pleasure and trivial matters connected to the worst crimes, we gain a large, unchanging, and steady joy. We also gain peace, Tranquility of mind, greatness, and gentleness. All cruelty is a sign of weakness.
