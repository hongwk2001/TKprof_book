[Chapter 6]
Our opponent says that the mind will also have its own Pleasure. Let the mind have its Pleasure if this is true. Let it judge luxury and Pleasure. Let it completely immerse itself in everything that gives sensual joy. Then let the mind look back at what it enjoyed before. The faded sensual Pleasure remains fresh in its memory. Let the mind joyfully expect another Pleasure that it felt long ago and plans to feel again. The body lies in weak fullness. Let the mind stretch its thoughts to the future and check its hopes while it does this. I think all these things will make the mind look much more miserable. Choosing evil instead of good is madness. A crazy person can never be happy. A person can never be sane if he thinks a harmful thing is the Highest Good and tries to get it. Therefore, a truly happy person can make the right judgment in all things. A person is happy if he is satisfied with his current situation no matter what it is. He is happy if he has a friendly relationship with the conditions of his life. His Reason supports and approves the direction of his whole situation. This person is exactly the happy person.
