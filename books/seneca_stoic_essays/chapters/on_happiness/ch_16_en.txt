[Chapter 16]
Therefore, true happiness lies in Virtue. What will this Virtue order you to do? It will order you not to think anything is bad or good if it has nothing to do with Virtue or vice. Next, it will order you to endure the attacks of evil without shaking. It will order you to treat good things like gods within the right limits. What reward does Virtue promise you for this war? It is a truly huge reward that raises you to the level of the gods. You will not be tied to any limits or poverty. You will be free, safe, and unhurt. You will not fail in anything you try, and you will not be left out of anything. Everything will happen according to your will. No bad luck will happen to you. Nothing will happen except what you expect and hope for. Does Virtue alone make you happy? Why not? This most perfect and divine Virtue is not just enough, it is more than enough. What could a person lack when he is put in a place where no desire can reach him? What more could he ask from the outside if everything he needs focuses inside himself? But a person who starts the journey toward Virtue still needs some help from fortune. This is true even if he makes great progress along the way. He is still struggling with purely human concerns. He tries to untie the knots and all the shackles that tie him to the mortal world. Then what is the difference between them? Some people are tied more tightly or loosely to these shackles, and some people even tie themselves. On the other hand, a person who makes progress toward the higher region and pulls himself up drags a much looser chain. He is basically free, even if he is not completely free yet.
