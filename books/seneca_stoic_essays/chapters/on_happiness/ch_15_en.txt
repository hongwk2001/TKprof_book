[Chapter 15]
Our opponent asks what stops Virtue and Pleasure from combining to form the Highest Good and making honor and Pleasure into one. Nothing can be part of honor except something honorable. The Highest Good loses its purity if it holds anything inside itself that does not look like its own better part. Even the joy that comes from Virtue is not part of the absolute good. Joy and peace of mind are truly good things. But they only follow the Highest Good and do not help complete it. They are just additions even though they come from the best causes. On the other hand, a person who makes a one-sided alliance between Virtue and Pleasure puts shackles on the strength of Virtue. He pushes freedom under a yoke. Freedom can only remain unconquered when it does not know anything more valuable than itself. Now he starts to need the help of fortune. This is the most perfect slavery. His life becomes anxious, full of doubt, fearful, and worried about unexpected accidents. If you make Virtue stand on unstable ground, you cannot provide a firm base for Virtue. What could be more unstable than depending on mere chance? How can a person shaken by the small pricks of Pleasure and pain obey God and happily accept everything that happens? How can he interpret everything that happens to him as good? A person driven by Pleasure cannot be a great defender of his country or a great protector of his friends. Therefore, let the Highest Good rise to a height where no force can shake it. Only Virtue can open the way to that place. You can only climb that hill with the help of Virtue. Virtue will bravely hold its position and gladly endure everything that happens to it. Virtue knows that all hard times come from the process of obeying the law of nature. Like a great soldier, Virtue will endure wounds and praise the general she serves while dying from a spear wound. Virtue will keep in mind the old saying to follow God. On the other hand, a person who grumbles and pities himself will still be forced to obey orders in the end. He will be dragged to do it no matter how much he does not want to. How crazy is it to be dragged instead of following? It is foolish to be sad about not having something or being treated harshly. It is foolish to be surprised or angry at diseases, death, and disasters that happen to both good and bad people. This shows ignorance of one's true place. Let us endure whatever the order of the universe requires us to carry with a generous mind. We are all bound by the oath to endure the evils that mortal life brings and happily submit to unavoidable things. We were born in a kingdom. Our freedom is to obey God.
