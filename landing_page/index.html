Deep Research Strategy

How we meticulously choose our output.

Our publishing decisions are not random. We employ an analytical approach to selecting which classic texts to modernize. Through comprehensive market gap analysis, we identify high-value public domain works that perfectly serve ESL learners and modern readers.

  • Market Gap Analysis: Identifying unmet needs in bilingual education.
  • Translation Methodology: Selecting the optimal modern phrasing while retaining original literary beauty.
  • Voice Synthesis Selection: Rigorously testing AI voice models against custom pronunciation rules to maximize emotional engagement and listening clarity.
  • Copyright & Adaptation: Sourcing verified public domain works and transforming them through careful retelling and modernization to ensure full copyright compliance and create uniquely original adaptations.
