# 📦 Amazon KDP Publishing Form Template & Guidelines

This document provides a reusable form template and decision guidelines for preparing books for the Amazon KDP Kindle Store.

---

## 💡 KDP metadata Guidelines

### 1. Title and Subtitle Formula
To maximize visibility and click-through rates, format all titles and subtitles using this high-performing template:
*   **KDP Book Title**: `[Classic Book Name]: Modernized Edition`
*   **KDP Subtitle**: `[Core Hook/Genre Description] ([Adaptation Type] for [Benefit 1], [Benefit 2], and [Benefit 3])`

### 2. Pricing Considerations
*   **70% Royalty Tier (Sweet Spot)**: Price the eBook between **$2.99 and $9.99** to qualify for Amazon's 70% royalty payout. Any price below $2.99 drops the royalty to 35%.
*   **Launch Strategy**: For new launches, you can price temporarily at **$0.99** (35% royalty) to lower barriers, generate search velocity, and collect early reviews, then raise it to $2.99+.
*   **Size & Length Value**: Price full-length novels (e.g., 40,000+ words like *Frankenstein*) at **$3.99 to $4.99**, while shorter essays or compilations (e.g., 10,000 to 20,000 words like *Stoic Essays* or *Art of War*) fit best at **$2.99**.
